# Saudi Wedding Vendor Prices (2024-2026, SAR): Kosha/Decor, Catering, Coffee Service, Cakes & Sweets — Eastern Province focus

> Method note (read first): research date 2026-11-09. Every vendor site I tried to open in full (valueevents.com.sa, hasselalbadiah.com, qahwajiyn.com, alyaum.com, haraj.com.sa) was blocked by this session's network settings. Those hosts would have to be allowed before the pages can be checked. So all the figures below come from search-engine extracts of the pages, not from full-page reads. The prices are as published on the listed pages, but I could not check each page's date or small print. Most pages show no publication date. "Published price" means a price shown on a vendor or ad page. "Estimate" means an aggregator, calculator or blog figure with no vendor behind it. Eastern Province (EP) data was thin, so Riyadh and Saudi-wide data fill the gaps and are labeled as such.

## (a) Kosha / stage decoration / event coordination / flowers — price range and units

### Takeaway
Kosha pricing runs from a few hundred riyals for a basic rented kosha (EP Haraj ads at about 800-880 SAR per day) to about 6,000 SAR for a mid-range custom kosha, and up to 19,000 SAR or more for luxury "queen" stages. Full coordination packages (kosha, aisle, sofas, tables, entrance) start at about 9,700 SAR. The unit is per event/day. Deposits are sometimes required.

### Inferences
- Working tiers (estimate, built from the figures above):
  - Economy: rented or basic kosha, 500-1,500 SAR.
  - Mid-range: custom kosha with aisle and flowers, 4,000-10,000 SAR.
  - Upper: full coordination package with kosha, aisle, sofas, entrance and tables, 10,000-20,000 SAR.
  - Luxury: 20,000-50,000+ SAR. The upper end of this range is extrapolated, because the highest published single-day kosha found was 19,000 SAR.
- Entrance flowers on their own start at about 400-500 SAR and grow with size and the share of natural flowers.

### Gaps

## (b) Catering & dinner — buffet per person, cooked sheep, totals

### Takeaway
Dammam halls that price per guest quote an open buffet at about 120-150 SAR per person. Calculator-style estimates run 50-150 SAR, with plated service 30-50% more. A cooked whole sheep with rice costs about 1,300-2,300 SAR from Saudi cooking kitchens, and a mufatah for 9-12 people about 750 SAR. A raw Naimi sheep alone costs about 2,000-2,670 SAR in 2025-26. One sheep feeds roughly 20-25 men in traditional service; heavier-portion guides say about 8-13 guests.

### Inferences
- Total catering cost estimates (my calculations from the cited unit prices):
  - **Buffet route, 120-150 SAR per person:** 300 guests ≈ 36,000-45,000 SAR; 500 guests ≈ 60,000-75,000 SAR.
  - **Economy buffet, about 60-80 SAR per person:** 300 ≈ 18,000-24,000; 500 ≈ 30,000-40,000.
  - **Luxury hotel, 200-300+ SAR per person:** 300 ≈ 60,000-90,000+; 500 ≈ 100,000-150,000+.
  - **Traditional sheep route, about 1 sheep per 20-25 guests at 1,300-2,300 SAR cooked with rice:**
    - 300 guests ≈ 12-15 sheep ≈ 16,000-35,000 SAR.
    - 500 guests ≈ 20-25 sheep ≈ 26,000-58,000 SAR.
    - Add salads, sweets and drinks on top.
    - The X post's 50,000 SAR dinner line is consistent with this range.
- Cooking-only fees run about 170-450 SAR per sheep when the family supplies the animal. The common cost-saving model is to buy a Naimi at about 2,000 SAR and pay a kitchen to cook it.

### Gaps
- No EP kitchen (Al-Sanousi, Walaem Al-Taahi, Al-Ahsa or Qatif kitchens) publishes an online per-sheep cooked price. Their Instagram and WhatsApp price lists could not be reached.
- No 2025-26 published per-person wedding buffet menu tiers were found for specific Khobar or Dammam hotels. The hotel figures are about 5 years old.

## (c) Coffee & tea service (صبابين / قهوجية / مباشرين), dates & sweets

One Riyadh site estimates 300-500 SAR per server for about 3 hours and 700-1,300 SAR for a 2-3 person crew. ### Gaps

## (d) Wedding cakes, sweets & chocolate

Saudi wedding cakes are priced per cake by size and servings, with the design added on top, rather than per tier or per kg. Published examples run about 395 SAR (serves 37) to 985 SAR (serves 45-50) for standard wedding cakes, and 720 SAR for a 3-tier cake at a Khobar bakery. Luxury designer cakes go far higher. ## Bundling with the venue (cross-category)

### Takeaway
In Dammam and Khobar, the bundle depends on the venue type. Some halls price per guest with the buffet included (120-150 SAR per person). Others sell section packages that include kosha, lighting, sound and hospitality. Others are hall-only, with no dinner. Hotels usually bundle dinner, hospitality drinks and sweets. Coffee servers are sometimes included in the men's section.

### Cited Findings
- Al-Bureik hall packages include kosha setup, lighting, sound, hospitality and buffet. Sultan Halls start from 19,000 SAR "including hospitality and tables". Al-Qasr for Events includes kosha but not dinner. — [zafaf.sa Dammam halls prices](https://zafaf.sa/%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AD-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85/); [Zafaf.net Al Qasr](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/al-qasr-for-events)
- Dammam Palace hotel provides hospitality: hot and cold drinks and sweets. — [Zafaf.net Dammam hotel prices](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/hotels/ideas/prices-wedding-venues-hotels-in-dammam-1454)
- A forum commenter says hall kitchens give lower quotes than independent kitchens. This is anecdotal. — [adslgate طبخ الولائم في الشرقية](https://www.adslgate.com/thread.php?t=2543481)

### Inferences
- Items usually bundled in hotel and premium-hall packages: dinner, hospitality drinks and sweets, basic kosha, tables and chairs, lighting and sound. Items usually bought separately: premium or custom kosha, fresh-flower entrances, the cake, chocolate favors, extra coffee servers, and traditional sheep dinners for men's sections (often from independent kitchens).

### Gaps
- No standard published EP hall inclusion sheet was found, so inclusions vary by venue and have to be confirmed in each quote.
