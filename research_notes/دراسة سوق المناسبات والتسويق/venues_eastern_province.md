# Wedding and Event Venue Prices, Eastern Province, Saudi Arabia (2024-2026, SAR)

> Research method note (Nov 2026): The session's network blocked direct page fetches for zafaf.sa, saudi-arabia.zafaf.net and gathern.co. Every figure below comes from search-engine extracts of those pages, not from reading the full pages. The date shown for each price is the page's own "last updated" date when the extract gave one; otherwise it is marked undated. Treat every figure as a listing or "starting from" price, not a contract quote. **Confirmed** = a price published on a venue or listing page. **Estimate** = a guide-level range or an inference.

## 1. Venue prices per night by type (lowest to highest)

### Takeaway
In the Eastern Province, a full wedding at a standalone hall or palace (both sections, men's and women's) usually starts around **SAR 14,000-30,000** at named mid-market Dammam and Jubail venues. Premium Dammam palaces list **SAR 60,000-73,000+**. Istirahas cost **SAR 500-8,000 per night**. Khobar and Dammam hotel ballrooms charge **per head**, roughly **SAR 120-500** per guest (one hotel up to SAR 950), so a 300-guest hotel wedding comes to about SAR 45,000-150,000.

### Inferences
- **Price ladder, lowest to highest (SAR per event night):** small istiraha 500-2,000 → large or two-section istiraha 3,000-8,000 → small-package hall (50-100 guests per section) 14,000-24,000 → mid-market full hall, both sections, 19,000-30,000 → Jubail and Khobar mid-to-upper halls 27,500-50,000 → premium Dammam and Khobar palaces 60,000-100,000+. Hotels: 3★ about 150-200 per head, 5★ about 250-400 per head, with premium packages up to 950 per head, plus about 15% service.
- 300-guest hotel example (estimate): 300 × SAR 250-400 = **SAR 75,000-120,000** before the about 15% service charge. Hotels are therefore often dearer than standalone halls for large weddings.
- Al-Ahsa looks like the cheapest hall market (from SAR 50 per head), and Khobar the most expensive. This is directional; the evidence is thin.
- Gathern's fees plus VAT (+24% over the base price) are a useful benchmark for the commission and fee take rate customers already accept in this category.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 published price lists from Khobar and Dammam 5-star hotels; hotel per-head figures are from an older (about 2020-21) aggregator article.
- No published prices for Qatif halls, Versailles Palace (Khobar), or Dhahran-specific venues.
- The full Zafaf and Gathern pages could not be read (network block), so package contents and exact dates could not be checked beyond the search extracts.

## 2. Weekday vs weekend, peak seasons (summer, after Eid), Ramadan

### Takeaway
Listings regularly say prices "differ on weekends and holidays", and hotels charge more on weekends. No Eastern Province source gives a numeric premium. Summer and after-Eid are known peaks in other Saudi cities, and Ramadan is generally a dead period for weddings, but these points are not quantified for the Eastern Province.

### Cited Findings
- Sultan halls (Dammam) and Al-Diyma istiraha: price varies on holidays and weekends. — [Zafaf.net Sultan](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/sultan-hall-dammam); [Zafaf.net Al-Diyma](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/villas/dammam/istirahat-al-diyma-for-events)
- Al-Humaidan (Jubail) says its price does **not** change on weekends or holidays. A counterexample, though the extract was internally inconsistent. — [Zafaf.net Al-Humaidan](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/aljubail/al-humaidan-hall-for-events)
- Khobar hotels: prices rise if the wedding is at the weekend. — [Zafaf.net hotel prices](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/hotels/ideas/prices-wedding-venues-in-alkhobar-hotels-1456)
- Guides say summer weddings are usually more expensive, and that prices rise in seasons such as Ramadan and official holidays (national guide, which may refer to post-Ramadan and Eid demand). — [Zafaf.sa 2025 guide](https://zafaf.sa/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AD/)
- Makkah: hall prices of SAR 15,000-80,000 per night, driven by mid-year school-holiday demand and early bookings. — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/IRIrJlh)
- Jeddah: "صيف جدة يلهب أسعار قاعات الأفراح" (summer inflates hall prices). — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1148332)
- Historic Eastern Province complaints about sudden rent increases and extra fees after midnight; residents called for price caps. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/article/585561)

### Inferences
- Thursday and Friday nights plus summer and post-Eid (Shawwal, Dhul-Hijjah) dates are the scarce inventory. Sun-Wed nights and Ramadan are the slack inventory a platform could discount.

### Gaps
- No quantified weekend or seasonal premium (for example, a percentage) for any Eastern Province venue. No Al-Yaum newspaper article on 2025-26 Eastern Province hall prices turned up in search.

## 3. Capacities (men's/women's sections) and price per capacity tier

### Takeaway
Typical Eastern Province halls have two sections of about 200-800 each. Small-package pricing (Al-Jawsaq, 2026) shows about SAR 14,000-17,000 for 50-100 men and SAR 19,000-24,000 for 50-100 women. The women's section is the dearer and decisive one; a women-only booking is cheaper than booking both sections.

### Cited Findings
- Al-Jawsaq Al-Sharqi (07.07.2026): 50 men 14k, 100 men 17k, 50 women 19k, 100 women 24k SAR. — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/al-jawsaq-wedding-halls-1103)
- Sultan (Dammam): 100-250 (Zafaf.net) vs 250-400 per section (Zafaf.sa). — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/sultan-hall-dammam)
- Versailles (Khobar): men 800, women 300+. — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/alkhobar/versailles-palace)
- Al-Andalusia (Jubail): 200+ per section. — [Qa3at](https://qa3at.com/sa/p/%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA/)
- Gathern two-section istiraha: 150 men's chairs plus 150+ women's chairs for SAR 6,000. — [Gathern](https://gathern.co/ar/view/27780/unit/39367)
- Hotels: Kempinski 800 reception; Mövenpick 150-600; voco about 300; Signature 450; Warwick 150; Park Inn 200; Khawali hall about 100. — sources in section 1.

### Inferences
- Rough per-guest economics: standalone halls work out at about SAR 50-150 per guest at 200-400 guests, while 5★ hotels cost SAR 250-400. Istirahas are about SAR 20 per guest at 300 guests but with minimal service.

### Gaps
- No published capacity-tier price ladder (for example, 300 vs 500 vs 800) for a single venue beyond Al-Jawsaq's small packages.

## 4. What is included vs extra

### Takeaway
Inclusions vary a lot. Some halls include dinner (Sultan, Al-Mashael); others exclude it (Al-Humaidan, Versailles, Khawali). Hotels include the buffet in the per-head price, then add about 15% service. Kosha, photography, special lighting and advanced decor are commonly extras. Coffee and tea service (coffee corner, coffee servers) is often bundled.

### Cited Findings
- Dinner included: Sultan halls, Al-Mashael. — [Zafaf.net Sultan](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/sultan-hall-dammam); [Zafaf.net Al-Mashael](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/mashael-hall-28604)
- Dinner not included, can book without dinner: Al-Humaidan (Jubail), Versailles and Al-Khawali (Khobar). Al-Khawali provides kosha and a tea and coffee corner. — [Al-Humaidan](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/aljubail/al-humaidan-hall-for-events); [Versailles](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/alkhobar/versailles-palace); [Al-Khawali](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/alkhobar/al-khawali-hall)
- Dammam guide: basic hospitality (buffet, drinks) is usually included, while advanced decor, kosha, photography and special lighting may be extras. — [Zafaf.sa Dammam](https://zafaf.sa/%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AD-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85/)
- Laylati hall (Qatif): packages include kosha, tea and coffee. — [Sobranews](https://www.sobranews.com/sobra/415909/)
- Hotels: per-head price with open buffet. Kempinski adds a free couple's suite. About 15% service is added on top. Zara Continental includes dinner and a kosha stage. — [Zafaf.net hotel prices](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/hotels/ideas/prices-wedding-venues-in-alkhobar-hotels-1456)
- Historic complaints about extra fees after midnight (overtime). — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/article/585561)

### Inferences
- A booking platform should standardise an inclusions checklist (dinner, coffee servers, kosha, sound/DJ, cleaning, overtime after midnight, service charge, VAT), because "from SAR X" headline prices are not comparable across venues.

### Gaps
- No source spelled out sound systems or cleaning as included or extra for an Eastern Province venue. No price list for add-ons (kosha, coffee servers) was found in this pass.

## 5. Deposit and cancellation norms

### Takeaway
There is no official Ministry of Commerce standard contract for voluntary cancellation found. Practice ranges from a non-refundable deposit to a 50% deduction even when the client cancels about 6 months ahead. Disputes go to the Ministry of Commerce consumer channel (1900 / "Balagh Tijari") or the courts. Specific Eastern Province deposit percentages were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Al-Watan: cancellations of wedding bookings range from a non-refundable deposit to a 50% deduction. One groom was charged 50% after cancelling about six months before the date. — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1186303)
- Published hall booking terms (example): the deposit is non-refundable if the client cancels, unless otherwise agreed in writing. — [Happiness Time terms](https://happiness-time.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AD%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%AC%D8%B2/)
- 2020: the Ministry of Commerce said customers were entitled to refunds from wedding halls and istirahas because of the official suspension of events. Complaints go to 1900 or the Balagh Tijari app. This applies only to cancellations forced by official decisions. — [MoC tweet](https://twitter.com/MCgovSA/status/1238844777380151296?lang=ar); [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/ORRnTs2)
- A January 2026 consumer case: a hotel refused to refund a hall deposit even though no contract was signed, only an invoice. — [Askmuhami on X](https://x.com/Askmuhami/status/2013640469473857943)
- Gathern's Dammam listing says no security deposit is required on arrival. — [Gathern](https://gathern.co/ar/view/57530/unit/88883)

### Inferences
- A clear, tiered, platform-backed cancellation policy (for example, a refund that shrinks with time to the event) would solve a real customer pain point. Customers face opaque terms today.

### Gaps
- No source gave a typical deposit percentage (for example, 20-50%) for Eastern Province halls. This needs primary calls or venue contracts.

## 6. Comparison with Riyadh, Jeddah and Makkah

### Takeaway
Eastern Province mid-market halls (about SAR 19,000-30,000) look broadly similar to or slightly below Riyadh's mid-market. Riyadh and Jeddah luxury palaces reach SAR 50,000-100,000+, while Riyadh hotel weddings run into the hundreds of thousands.

### Cited Findings
