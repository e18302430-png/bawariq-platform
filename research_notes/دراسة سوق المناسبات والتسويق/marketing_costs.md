Research date: 2026-11-09. Currency: SAR. USD amounts are converted at 3.75.

## 1. Platform usage and reach in Saudi Arabia (penetration, demographics, women vs men)

### Takeaway
Snapchat and TikTok are the two biggest ad platforms in KSA, and both are used more by women than by men. Snapchat is the most-used app among Saudi women after WhatsApp (88.2% of female internet users), which makes it the main channel for brides and families. Instagram reaches far fewer people (about 18 million adults) but still matters for visual wedding inspiration.

### Cited Findings
- **Snapchat, CST "Saudi Internet 2025" report [CONFIRMED, regulator survey]:** Snapchat is the second most-used app in KSA. Usage is **88.2% among women vs 80.2% among men**. — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/664123); same figures in [Saudi Expatriates (Aug 2026)](https://www.saudi-expatriates.com/2026/08/most-used-apps-in-saudi-arabia.html)
- Snapchat reaches **83.6% of internet users** in KSA (CST Saudi Internet 2025, as relayed on LinkedIn). — [LinkedIn post citing CST](https://sa.linkedin.com/in/shaden-alangari) (secondary relay)
- **TikTok, same CST report [CONFIRMED]:** usage is **77.6% among women vs 76.5% among men**. — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/664123)
- **Snapchat ad reach, DataReportal [CONFIRMED, Kepios]:** about **24.7 million** in KSA, the 4th-largest Snapchat audience in the world after the US, Pakistan and France. Late-2025 estimates are 24.7-25.3 million. — [DataReportal Snapchat stats](https://datareportal.com/essential-snapchat-stats)
- **TikTok ad reach, DataReportal Digital 2026 (late-2025 data) [CONFIRMED]:** **38.6 million adults (18+)**, up 5.81 million (+17.7%) over the year. That equals **154.3% of adults**, because ad tools count accounts, not unique people. The January 2025 edition gave 34.1 million (138.2%). The Digital 2026 Mid-Year update gives 164%. — [Voctos summary of DataReportal](https://voctos.net/social-media-ad-reach-saudi-uae-2026/); [Statista (Oct 2025)](https://statista.com/statistics/1299829/tiktok-penetration-worldwide-by-country); [DataReportal mid-year 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mid-year-global-update-report)
- **Instagram ad reach, DataReportal Digital 2026 (Oct 2025 data) [CONFIRMED]:** **18.2 million (71.0% of adults)**, against Facebook at 17.7 million. The January 2025 figure was 16.9 million. — [Voctos summary](https://voctos.net/social-media-ad-reach-saudi-uae-2026/); [Marketing House](https://marketinghouseeg.com/latest-social-media-trends-in-saudi-arabia/)
- Instagram usage penetration: **72.7% (about 18.78 million)** in 2024. — [Global Media Insight](https://www.globalmediainsight.com/blog/saudi-arabia-social-media-statistics/)
- **Facebook gender skew:** 75.7% of Facebook's adult ad audience in KSA is male. — [adjoltz (citing DataReportal Nov 2025)](https://adjoltz.com/blog/saudi-arabia-social-media-statistics/) [unverified secondary source]
- **Instagram gender split:** 42.4% of women vs 39.5% of men, per one secondary source. — [adjoltz](https://adjoltz.com/blog/saudi-arabia-social-media-statistics/). This is not verified, and it conflicts with the 72.7% overall-usage figure above, so it probably measures something else (daily use?).
- An older Talkwalker page puts Snapchat KSA users at 51% female and 47% male (more than 2.5 years old). — [Talkwalker](https://www.talkwalker.com/blog/social-media-statistics-saudi-arabia)
- Snap opened a Saudi office and creator hub, which signals the market's strategic weight for Snap. — [AGBI, Dec 2024](https://agbi.com/analysis/media/2024/12/snap-snapchat-saudi-arabia-office-creator-hub-mena-social-media)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** For brides and mothers, the female skew on Snapchat (+8 points over men) and TikTok makes these the main channels. Instagram is a secondary inspiration and portfolio channel, which matters most for showcasing vendors (halls, photographers, kosha designers).
- **[INFERENCE]** Facebook is male-skewed, so Meta budget should go to Instagram placements (Reels and Stories) rather than Facebook.

### Gaps
- No province-level data for the Eastern Province (للمنطقة الشرقية) from DataReportal or CST.
- The gender split of the Instagram ad audience in KSA could not be verified (DataReportal was blocked).

## 2. Minimum ad spend and CPM / CPC / CPI / CPL benchmarks per platform in KSA

### Takeaway
None of the three platforms publishes KSA rate cards, so every Saudi CPM and CPC figure is an agency estimate, and the sources differ by a factor of two to nine. Directionally, Snapchat and TikTok are the cheapest per 1,000 impressions (about SAR 5-18), Instagram is roughly two times more expensive (about SAR 18-35), and Ramadan adds 50-90%.

### Cited Findings
**Platform minimums** (global platform rules; third-party blogs, not checked against official docs):
- **Snapchat:** a **$5/day (SAR ~19) minimum** per Ad Squad. Snapchat recommends **$20-50/day (SAR 75-190)**. — [Stackmatix 2026](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/minimum-daily-budget-by-platform-2026); [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/faq-items/how-much-for-a-day-of-advertising-on-snapchat/)
- **TikTok:** **$50 (SAR ~188) campaign-level minimum** and **$20/day (SAR 75) per ad group**. — [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/minimum-daily-budget-by-platform-2026); [Coinis](https://coinis.com/how-to/start-tiktok-ads-with-small-budget)
- **Meta (Instagram):** a technical minimum of about **$1/day**, but the practical minimum is **$20-50/day**. The rule of thumb is a daily budget of at least 5 times the target CPA per ad set. — [TryVizup 2026](https://www.tryvizup.com/blog/meta-ads-minimum-budget-requirements-2026); [Extuitive](https://extuitive.com/articles/meta-ads-minimum-budget-for-testing)

**Snapchat KSA** [AGENCY EST.]:
- Blended CPM is **SAR 6-18 ($1.60-4.80)**. CPC (swipe-up) is **SAR 0.80-4.50 ($0.21-1.20)**. — [23HubLab](https://23hublab.com/snapchat-advertising-performance-in-saudi-arabia/)
- CPC is $0.15-0.60 (SAR 0.56-2.25). — [Hovi 2026](https://thehovi.com/blog/agency-insights/performance-marketing-ksa-uae-strategies-roi-2026)
- Published KSA CPM estimates range from **SAR 6 to SAR 56** (about a nine-fold spread). Snap publishes no rate card. — [Popcorn Studio 2026](https://www.popcornstudio.ai/blog/snapchat-ads-cost-saudi-arabia)
- BIMO refuses to give a precise Saudi Snapchat CPM: "any article quoting you a precise Saudi Snapchat CPM is quoting its own ad account." — [BIMO 2026](https://www.bimogroup.co/blog/snapchat-ads-saudi-gcc-benchmark)
- KSA CPC by objective: **app installs SAR 1.50-5.00**, **lead generation SAR 2.00-6.00**. — [23HubLab](https://23hublab.com/meta-ads-benchmarks-across-gcc-markets/)
- Global Snapchat comparison: CPC median $0.84. CPM medians run from $5.84 (awareness) to $27.10 (conversions). App-install CPM median is $23.40 (IQR $14.90-35.20). — [Whatagraph](https://whatagraph.com/blog/articles/ppc-benchmarks); [AdLiftr 2026](https://adliftr.com/blog/snapchat-ads-cost-benchmarks-2026)

**TikTok KSA** [AGENCY EST.]:
- CPM is **SAR 4.50-9.00 ($1.20-2.40)**. — [23HubLab via search extract](https://23hublab.com/meta-ads-benchmarks-across-gcc-markets/)
- CPC is $0.20-0.80 (SAR 0.75-3.00). Hovi's table columns are not clearly labelled. — [Hovi](https://thehovi.com/blog/agency-insights/performance-marketing-ksa-uae-strategies-roi-2026)
- KSA TikTok CPMs are "moderate-high, typically below Meta". Most published figures are US-centric. — [Pixelmovers 2026](https://pixelmovers.co/blog/tiktok-ads-benchmarks-2026)
- Global TikTok comparison (Triple Whale FY2025 ecommerce): CTR 1.77%, **CPM $13.26**, CVR 2.01%, CPA $32.74. App-install CPM is $12.40, about 30% cheaper than Meta. — [Whatagraph](https://whatagraph.com/blog/articles/ppc-benchmarks); [AdLiftr](https://adliftr.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-benchmarks-2026)

**Instagram / Meta KSA** [AGENCY EST.]:
- **Instagram CPM is SAR 18-35**. Facebook CPM is SAR 15-28. — [23HubLab](https://23hublab.com/meta-ads-benchmarks-across-gcc-markets/)
- Meta KSA CPM is **$8-15 (SAR 30-56)**. A July 2026 public dataset gives KSA at **$12.01 (SAR 45)**. — [BIMO Meta GCC 2026](https://www.bimogroup.co/blog/meta-ads-benchmarks-gulf)
- Another guide puts Meta at SAR 8-14 CPM. This conflicts with the figures above. — [23HubLab (other page)](https://23hublab.com/snapchat-advertising-performance-in-saudi-arabia/)

**CPI (app install), KSA** [AGENCY EST., single vertical]:
- For voice-chat apps in KSA: **Snapchat $1.50-4.00 (SAR 5.6-15)**, **Meta $3-7 (SAR 11-26)**, Google App Campaigns $4-9. — [Voxire 2026](https://voxire.com/blog/snapchat-advertising-lebanon-mena-2026/)
- Global TikTok CPI: gaming $2.20, utility $4.40. — [AdLiftr](https://adliftr.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-benchmarks-2026)

**Ad formats:**
- No KSA-specific prices were found for Snapchat Story or Collection ads, TikTok Spark Ads or Instagram Reels ads. All of them run on the same auction, so the CPMs above apply.

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE] Working assumptions for budget modelling (mid-case):**
  - Snapchat: CPM SAR 12, swipe-up CPC SAR 1.5-3, CPI SAR 10-15, cost per registration SAR 15-35.
  - TikTok (incl. Spark Ads): CPM SAR 8-15, CPC SAR 1.5-3, CPI SAR 10-20.
  - Instagram Reels and Stories: CPM SAR 25-45, CPC SAR 2.5-5, CPI SAR 15-26.
  - Lead or signup cost in the narrow wedding niche will probably be **SAR 20-60 per registered bride or family**. That is extrapolated from the CPC × assumed 5-15% landing-to-signup conversion; it is not a published figure.
- **[INFERENCE]** Geo-targeting the Eastern Province (Dammam, Khobar, Dhahran, Qatif, Al-Ahsa) shrinks the audience, so CPMs may run higher than the national blends. Expect frequency to saturate quickly at higher budgets.
- **[INFERENCE]** At the minimums, running Snap, TikTok and IG test campaigns together needs about SAR 225-570/day, which is **SAR 7,000-17,000/month** just to clear the "recommended" learning thresholds.

### Gaps
- No official Snap, TikTok or Meta KSA rate cards exist publicly.
- No published KSA benchmark for cost-per-lead or signup in the events or wedding vertical.
- No public Saudi case studies on Snapchat or TikTok for Business for event or wedding apps were found.

## 3. Influencer (مشاهير) prices in Saudi Arabia by tier and platform; wedding/beauty; Eastern Province

Vendor guides agree broadly on these ranges per post or package: nano SAR 500-2,500, micro SAR 2,000-15,000, macro SAR 10,000-75,000, and mega above SAR 75,000-100,000, up to SAR 500,000+. ## 4. Mawthooq (موثوق) license: requirement, penalties, brand obligations

### Takeaway
Any individual paid to advertise on social media in KSA needs a Mawthooq license from GCAM (هيئة الإعلام): SAR 15,000 for 3 years, issued through the Ilam platform. Brands are told not to contract unlicensed advertisers, and non-Saudi influencers face especially heavy penalties. Practical rule for مناسبتي: only book influencers who show a valid Mawthooq number, and keep the record.

### Cited Findings
- The license costs **SAR 15,000 (~$4,000)** and is valid for **3 years**. It is issued through the Ilam platform. Non-Saudis must be represented by approved advertising agencies. — [Arab News (GCAM rules)](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2176661/media); [Arab News explainer](https://arab.news/2xun3)
- Contracting an advertising agency does not replace the license. It covers the main platforms and also applies to advertisers outside KSA. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2171304)
- Promoting your own work on a personal account needs no license. Commercial promotion for others does. — [Arab News](https://arab.news/g5dhn)
- **Brands:** authorities told companies **not to contract with, or invite to marketing events, non-Saudi advertisers who break the rules**, unless they operate under a licensed commercial entity. — [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://aawsat.com/home/article/3837116/%D8%AD%D8%B8%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%B1-%C2%AB%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%C2%BB-%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%AE%D9%8A%D8%B5-%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9); [Asharq Al-Awsat English](https://english.aawsat.com/node/3793846)
- **Penalties:**
  - Violations can bring a warning, a **fine up to SAR 1 million**, and/or a ban. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2140406/media)
  - Non-Saudis advertising without a license (an "economic activity" violation) face up to **SAR 5 million and 5 years in prison**. — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/thehour/a-fine-of-5-million-riyals-preventing-non-saudi-celebrities-from-advertising-without-a-license)
  - In 2022 an Arab visitor was fined **SAR 400,000** and banned. — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/gulf-news/saudi-arabia/2022/08/01/saudi-arabia-launches-mawthooq-platform-for-individual-advertising-on-social-media)
  - Residents were fined SAR 300,000 for unlicensed ads. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2171304)
  - Snapchat celebrities have been fined about **SAR 100,000** with license suspension or cancellation. — [sadasaudi.net](https://sadasaudi.net/10971/); [mz-mz.net (2026)](https://mz-mz.net/1801509/)
- Licensees must remove content immediately on GCAM request. They are responsible for their content and must confirm the advertised products are licensed by SASO. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2176661/media)
- Paid content must be disclosed, and Saudi creators doing paid promotion need a GCAM permit. — [Hypebox](https://hypebox.me/blog/articles/how-to-find-the-best-ugc-and-influencer-creators-in-saudi-arabia-(riyadh-edition))

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** UGC creators whose videos run only from the brand's own ad account may sit in a grey zone. Many Saudi UGC marketplaces still require licensing or disclosure, so confirm with legal counsel.
- **[INFERENCE]** Budget for a 15% VAT on top of influencer fees when the creator is VAT-registered. UGCeer explicitly adds VAT (see section 6).

### Gaps
- No official GCAM text naming a specific fine on the *brand* that contracts an unlicensed Saudi influencer.
- No information on whether the Mawthooq fee or rules changed in 2025-2026. Searches only surfaced Iraq, Jordan and UAE changes.

## 5. Recommended budget split and seasonality (wedding season, Eid, Ramadan)

### Takeaway
No published source gives a budget split specific to an events or wedding app in KSA. Seasonality is the bigger lever: Ramadan CPMs run about 50-90% above baseline, and Eid and National Day add 30-60%. Wedding demand peaks in the summer holiday and right after Eid, and halls are booked 6-8 months ahead, so the acquisition push should land in the planning window before the peak.

### Cited Findings
- **Ramadan CPMs:**
  - **60-90% above baseline**. — [Hovi](https://thehovi.com/blog/agency-insights/performance-marketing-ksa-uae-strategies-roi-2026)
  - Ramadan Meta CPM in KSA is **SAR 35-70** (50-80% above normal). — [23HubLab](https://23hublab.com/meta-ads-benchmarks-across-gcc-markets/)

## 6. Agency fees and content production (UGC) costs in Saudi Arabia

### Takeaway
Saudi UGC video costs run from SAR 150 (marketplace floor) to about SAR 2,250 per video at the mid tier. Managed UGC campaigns start at about SAR 15,000, and an account-manager service costs SAR 1,999/month. No public data was found on Saudi agency retainers. Freelance campaign setup on Khamsat is very cheap ($5-15), but that only covers management, not ad spend.

### Cited Findings
- **Saudi/UAE UGC per video (2026) [AGENCY EST.]:**
  - Nano **$80-200 (SAR 300-750)**.
  - Micro **$200-600 (SAR 750-2,250)**.
  - Mid-tier **$600-2,000 (SAR 2,250-7,500)**.
  - Source: [Gravity Media](https://gravity-media.net/en/blog/ugc-content-cost-2026)
- **UGCeer (Saudi marketplace) [vendor list price]:** minimum **SAR 150 per video** plus commission plus 15% VAT. Account manager is **SAR 1,999/month**. Managed campaigns start at **SAR 15,000**. — [ugceer.com](https://ugceer.com/en/); [UGCeer landing](https://ugceer.com/en/land)
- **PushBrands:** from SAR 150, with SAR 100 stated elsewhere on the same site. — [PushBrands](https://pushbrands.app/en/blog/saudi-ugc-product-launch-14-day-plan); [PushBrands](https://pushbrands.app/en/blog/saudi-ugc-creator-profiles-verified-metrics)
- **Creator-side pay:** SAR 300-1,500 per video in direct deals. One platform pays SAR 1,500 per authorised video. — [Mabrook UGC](https://www.mabrookugc.com/blog/how-to-become-ugc-creator)
- **UAE comparison:** AED 600-2,000 per video. — [Influencer Metric](https://influencermetric.com/blog/ugc-rates-2026-us-uk-gulf)
- **Freelance Snapchat campaign setup and management on Khamsat:** about $5-15 per campaign or a few days (ad budget paid separately). — [Khamsat](https://khamsat.com/marketing/snapchat-ads/1722999-%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%A9-4-%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%85%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%84-5-%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B1)

### Inferences

- **[INFERENCE]** Creative fatigue on Snap and TikTok needs about 8-15 new creatives a month at the launch tier, which is about **SAR 3,000-8,000/month** with SAR 300-750 UGC videos.
