# Saudi Wedding Vendor Prices (Eastern Province focus, Saudi-wide fallback) and Total Wedding Cost Tiers

Research date: 2026-11-09. All prices are in SAR. Method note: these findings come mostly from search-result snippets and vendor listing pages. Full-page fetches failed because many Saudi hosts (al-madina.com, okaz.com.sa, hijri-calendars.com, ahsebli.com, ehsabi.com, arabiaweddings.com) are blocked by this session's network allowlist. Treat each figure as "listed/advertised price", not a verified transaction price, unless it is marked otherwise. Many listings carry no date, and some are more than 3 years old; these are flagged.

## 1. Photography and videography (women's section, men's section, drone, same-day edit / زفة video)

### Takeaway
In the Eastern Province, coverage for the women's section runs from about 600 SAR (small Qatif all-female teams) to about 2,500 SAR at the entry and mid level. Saudi-wide premium women's photographers charge 5,000-9,000 SAR, and full multi-crew luxury coverage is estimated at up to 30,000 SAR or more. A drone typically adds 1,000-1,500 SAR, and a زفة/cinematic video adds about 1,500 SAR.

### Cited Findings
- Qatif (Eastern Province): the women's photographer "أسماء فيديو" lists packages from 600 to 2,500 SAR, with a fully female crew. — [asmaa.video/qatif](https://asmaa.video/qatif)
- Kamar Studio (Saudi): women's photography packages start at 2,500 SAR for 3 hours. Drone add-on is 1,000 SAR, and a زفة video added to any package is 1,500 SAR. — [kamarstudio.sa](https://kamarstudio.sa/%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%AA/)
- Ziyad Art: a drone package (cinematic videographer plus aerial videographer) costs 3,500 SAR, and a photos-only package costs 1,150 SAR. — [ziyad-studio.com](https://ziyad-studio.com/)
- Azzam Studio, men's events packages: 1,300 SAR for comprehensive coverage, 1,500 SAR for aerial video, and 350 SAR for a groom pre-wedding session. — [azzamstudio.co](https://www.azzamstudio.co/weddingspackages)
- Makkah/Jeddah women's studios, via Zafaf.net (more than 5 years old): Abeer Khairat album packages cost 3,500-9,000 SAR and video starts at 1,500 SAR. Amal Ahmed charges 6,000-8,000 SAR in Makkah and 5,000-7,000 SAR in Jeddah. — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/photographers-and-videographers/ideas/women-photography-studio-in-mecca-1602)
- OpenSooq ad: a women's wedding photographer advertises prices "starting from 1,500 SAR". The city is unclear. — [OpenSooq](https://sa.opensooq.com/ar/search/280400981)
- Blog estimate: wedding photography starts at 5,000 SAR and reaches 30,000 SAR or more at luxury halls. — [hayamgomaa.net](https://hayamgomaa.net/%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%84%D9%81%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84/). The ehsabi budget calculator uses about 12,000 SAR as a mid-option default. — [ehsabi.com](https://ehsabi.com/c/wedding-budget-ksa/) (snippet only)
- A personal budget posted on X (April 2025) lists the photographer at 4,000 SAR. — [X @malmuqti](https://x.com/malmuqti/status/1908619732602810574?lang=ar)
- Other listings exist without visible prices: FATENGRAPHY booking ([fatengraphy.com](https://www.fatengraphy.com/booking/project-one-sdjxd)), 7or Studio packages ([7orstudio.cfd](https://7orstudio.cfd/packages.html)), and Haraj and mstaml ads.

### Inferences
- Working tiers for the Eastern Province women's section: economy 600-1,500 SAR, mid 2,500-5,000 SAR, premium 6,000-12,000 SAR (photo, video and album). A full luxury setup (women's and men's crews, drone, cinema, same-day edit) costs about 15,000-30,000 SAR.
- Men's-section coverage appears cheaper than the women's (about 1,300-1,500 SAR entry), likely because demand is lower and male photographers are more available.
- Pricing units are per event/night in hourly blocks (for example 3 hours), with add-ons priced per item (drone, زفة video, extra hours, travel outside the city at about +1,000 SAR).

### Gaps
- I found no explicit "same-day edit" price. The closest proxy is the 1,500 SAR زفة video add-on.
- I found no confirmed price lists for named Dammam/Khobar women's photographers. Most work through Instagram/WhatsApp DMs.

## 2. Sound, lighting and LED screens (per night)

### Takeaway
Rentals of LED walls and screens are cheap at the unit level (about 265-500 SAR/day for a small 3x2 m wall or a single screen). Event lighting runs from 300 to 3,000+ SAR per day. A full wedding stage package (sound, lighting and LED) is not well documented online.

### Cited Findings
- Jad Services guide: a ready LED wall of about 3x2 m rents for 333-450 SAR, and a single 50-65" screen with stand rents for 450-500 SAR. — [sa.jad.services](https://sa.jad.services/blog/post/%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%B6-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9)
- Estajer rental platform: an 86" interactive screen in Dammam costs 1,380 SAR/day, an outdoor LED P3.95 costs 322 SAR/day (pickup in Khobar), and Riyadh LED screens cost about 265-300 SAR/day. — [estajer.com](https://estajer.com/en/parties-and-events/screens/products)
- ZONEX 2026 guide: event lighting rental costs 300 to 3,000+ SAR per day depending on the setup. Sound and LED are usually ordered together with lighting. — [zonexsa.com](https://zonexsa.com/blog/%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A5%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%81%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3/)
- Haraj and mstaml carry Dammam screen and stage rental ads, but the visible price ("1 SAR") is a placeholder. — [mstaml Dammam](https://www.mstaml.com/sa/product/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B7%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85/%D8%AA%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A3%D8%AE%D8%B1%D9%89/%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88-%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D8%A8%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B6%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85?id=4360765&location=34&type=2.24), [Haraj](https://haraj.com.sa/en/search/%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1/)

### Inferences
- The unit-level figures above (per screen or wall, per day) are for small setups. Large wedding LED walls (for example 6x3 m or bigger) plus a technician would plausibly cost several thousand SAR. This is an inference, not sourced.
- Many halls include basic sound and lighting. The external spend is usually DJ sound, extra LED, and زفة effects.

### Gaps
- I found no sourced price for a full wedding-night package covering sound, lighting and a large LED wall in the Eastern Province. Quotes are needed.

## 3. DJ, طقاقات, folk bands (فرق شعبية / عرضة) and زفة

### Takeaway
DJs are the cheapest item (about 350-1,500 SAR per night). Recorded زفة tracks cost 200-5,000 SAR, with custom-name tracks reaching up to 18,000 SAR. طقاقات are the expensive item: older press reports put them at 6,000-13,000 SAR, VIP above 20,000 SAR, and star طقاقات at 30,000-48,000 SAR per night. I found no 2025-dated طقاقة rate.

### Cited Findings
- Okaz (older article, date not visible): the cheapest third-tier طقاقة performs a wedding for 6,000 SAR, the range by tier is 6,000-13,000 SAR, and VIP exceeds 20,000 SAR per night. One groom reported paying over 9,000 SAR because of modern instruments and staff. — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/article/424160)
- Al-Watan (older): one طقاقة charges 39,000 SAR per night inside Jeddah and 48,000 SAR outside it, with travel and lodging paid by the family. In other cities the condition is 30,000 SAR. — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/141503)
- Recorded زفة, Eastern Province (زفة المساء, Zafaf.net): a ready-made زفة with names costs up to 1,000 SAR, and a new زفة with names costs up to 5,000 SAR. — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/band-dj-and-entertainment/ashsharqiah/zaffet-al-masaa-30789)
- Riyadh (ليالي العروسة): a ready-made name زفة costs 500 SAR, custom-name زفات cost 1,000-18,000 SAR depending on the singer, and custom lyrics and melody cost 3,000-5,000 SAR. — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/band-dj-and-entertainment/riyadh/zaffat-and-shelat-layali-al-aroose-30808)
- Farhat Al-Omr price list (more than 3 years old): name زفات from 1,200 SAR, non-name versions from 200 SAR. — [farhtomr.com](https://farhtomr.com/service-prices/)
- Outlier: a Saudi trainer says a full staged زفة production (including عرضة) can reach 200,000 SAR. — [nativitytv.ps](https://nativitytv.ps/29568.html)
- DJ: Riyadh packages are 800, 1,000 and 1,500 SAR (including speakers and a female operator) per a TikTok listing. Makkah/Jeddah/Taif DJs start from 350 SAR, and Haraj Jeddah ads start from 500 SAR. A live female instrumental band was quoted at 3,250 SAR. — [dyaftkm.com](https://dyaftkm.com/product/%D8%AF%D9%8A-%D8%AC%D9%8A-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA/), [Haraj](https://haraj.com.sa/en/112616858/%D8%AF%D9%8A_%D8%AC%D9%8A_%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AD_%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%87_%D8%A8%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%B1_500_%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84_%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7_%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%AC%D8%B2_/), [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/%D9%83%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%8A-%D8%AC%D9%8A)
- Folk/عرضة troupe: the only explicit package found was Kuwaiti (15-person team with زفة and عرضة for 300 KWD, about 3,600 SAR at roughly 12.2 SAR/KWD). It is not Saudi. — [hostfarah.com](https://hostfarah.com/products/%D8%AF%D8%B3%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86)

### Inferences
- Present the طقاقة figures as "press-reported, older". Current Eastern Province rates are likely similar or higher, but I could not confirm that.
- Pricing units: DJ and طقاقة are per night, with travel and lodging extra for out-of-city bookings. زفة is a one-off production per track.

### Gaps
- I found no 2024-2026 dated طقاقة or Saudi عرضة troupe rate for Dammam, Khobar or Al-Ahsa.

## 4. Invitations (electronic vs printed)

### Takeaway
E-invitations cost 25-205 SAR flat for a design or app package, or about 2.5 SAR per guest on RSVP platforms. Printed cards cost about 8-35 SAR per card in Riyadh and 10-250 SAR per card in Jeddah for luxury boxed cards (older data).

### Cited Findings
- Haraj freelance e-invite designers charge 25 SAR per design, and another charges 35 SAR (+50 SAR for custom work, delivered in 30 minutes). — [Haraj](https://haraj.com.sa/en/1175164653/%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%85_%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A9_%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/), [Haraj](https://haraj.com.sa/en/1191619518/%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%85_%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%B1_%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA_%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC/)
- da3wa.online RSVP platform: the gold package is from 2.50 SAR per guest, the basic e-invite is 51 SAR, and Pro is 205 SAR flat. — [da3wa.online](https://da3wa.online/%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B2%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9?lang=en)
- Zafaf.net (more than 5 years old): WhatsApp/email e-invites from about 40 SAR and video invitations from 100 SAR. Printed luxury cards cost 8-35 SAR per card in Riyadh and 10-250 SAR in Jeddah, usually including design and envelope. — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-invitations/ideas/how-to-define-wedding-invitation-prices-1651)
- Other e-invite and template providers: Octobox ([octobox.sa](https://octobox.sa/lYKEqe)), Takareer ([takareer.com](https://takareer.com/pages/wedding-cards-templates)), and Canva print from 9 USD ([canva.com](https://www.canva.com/wedding-invitations/templates/)).

### Inferences
- For 300-500 printed cards at 8-35 SAR each, the total is about 2,400-17,500 SAR. An e-invite with RSVP for 500 guests at 2.5 SAR each is about 1,250 SAR. E-invites are therefore 5-10x cheaper, and they are now the default for many Saudi weddings.

### Gaps
- I found no Eastern Province printer price lists dated 2024-2026.

Another listing is 300 SAR total including cleaning. — [sharikni.com](https://sharikni.com/product/318/%D9%81%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%B2%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1)

- "فستان الدمام" on Zafaf.net (updated November 2024): minimum 1,500 SAR, and offers white wedding dress rental. — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/haute-couture/dammam/fst-n-ldm-m)

## 6. Makeup and hair (مكياج وتسريحة)

### Takeaway
This is the best-documented category. Bridal makeup in Dammam and Khobar costs about 1,000-2,000 SAR at salons listed on Fresha, VIP packages cost up to about 2,700 SAR, and home service adds a premium (for example 1,300 vs 1,700 SAR).

### Cited Findings (Fresha listings, current at search time)
- Dammam: Lara Note Spa bride packages cost 1,400-1,800 SAR, and VIP costs 2,700 SAR. Sage Salon charges 1,500 SAR for bridal makeup and hair. Kholoud Beauty charges 2,000 SAR for a bride package. 99 Beauty Salon charges 1,699 SAR, or 1,899 SAR for VIP. An aggregator says Dammam bridal makeup starts from 1,000 SAR. — [Fresha Dammam](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/tt/bridal-makeup/in/sa-addammam)
- Khobar: Reset Salon charges 1,000 SAR (about 2 hours). Tiara Beauty Studio charges 1,300 SAR in salon and 1,700 SAR for home service. Elite Sense charges 1,200 SAR. Beautiholic charges 670 SAR for a "royal bride" package. Khobar bridal makeup is said to cost up to 1,800 SAR. — [Fresha Khobar](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/tt/bridal-makeup/in/sa-alkhubar/al-khobar)

### Inferences
- Celebrity freelance makeup artists on Instagram, who are commonly reported to charge 3,000-6,000+ SAR, are not captured on Fresha. That premium range is unverified.

### Gaps

## 10. Total Saudi wedding cost (economy, mid, luxury), budget breakdown, and dowry

### Takeaway
There is no current official total. GASTAT's often-cited figure (about 12,500 SAR, May 2022, up 32% from 9,464 SAR) measures only the wedding-event/party service component. All-in planning estimates (dowry, hall, catering, gold, furniture, honeymoon) cluster at about 80,000 SAR economy, 150,000-200,000 SAR average, and 300,000-500,000+ SAR luxury.

### Inferences: budget split (derived from the X itemized budget and the calculators; estimates)
- Using the about 186,000 SAR itemized budget: catering and hospitality (dinner, sweets, hospitality) are about 36%, dowry about 22%, furniture about 22%, hall about 13%, kosha and decor about 3%, photography about 2%, and car and hotel about 2%.
- Tier model for the party/vendor side only, excluding dowry, furniture and gold. This is my synthesis from the categories above:
  - Economy (about 20,000-40,000 SAR): rest house or modest hall, e-invites, DJ about 1,000 SAR, salon makeup about 1,000 SAR, rented dress about 500-2,000 SAR, photographer about 1,000-2,500 SAR.
  - Mid (about 60,000-120,000 SAR): hall 25,000 SAR, dinner about 50,000 SAR, photographer 4,000-8,000 SAR, mid-tier طقاقة 6,000-13,000 SAR, makeup about 1,500-2,000 SAR, purchased dress 3,000-9,000 SAR, giveaways 2,000-5,000 SAR.
  - Luxury (about 200,000-500,000+ SAR): hotel hall above 100,000 SAR, VIP طقاقة 20,000-48,000 SAR, full photo and cinema crews 15,000-30,000 SAR, couture dress up to 35,000 SAR, luxury car 1,500-12,000 SAR/day, custom زفة up to 18,000 SAR.
- Vendor categories in this file (photo, entertainment, beauty, dress, invites, giveaways, car) together are about 10-25% of total marriage spend. Hall and catering dominate the party budget.
