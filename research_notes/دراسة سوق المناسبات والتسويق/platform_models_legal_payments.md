# Platform Revenue Models, Saudi Legal Requirements, and Payment Gateways for "مناسبتي" (as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-09. Method: web search (some hosts, e.g. ijjad.com and grantthornton.sa, could not be fetched from this session's network, so a few findings rely on search-result summaries rather than full-page reads). Nothing here is legal or tax advice. Figures marked "estimate" or "secondary" should be checked with the provider or authority before use.

## Q1. What do Saudi/GCC event, venue and service platforms charge?

### Takeaway
Saudi event and venue platforms do not publish commission rates. The only Saudi-adjacent figures are a Gathern host's unverified complaint that commission "reaches 30%" and about 20% for Arabic freelance marketplaces (Khamsat; Mostaql up to 20%). Generic venue-marketplace guidance puts venue-side commission at 15-20%, and event-vendor marketplaces at 5-15%.

### Cited Findings
- **Gathern (جاذر إن):** no official host commission page was found. A host review on the Gathern for Business App Store listing says the commission is excessive and "reaches 30% of profits". This is anecdotal and unverified. — [App Store: جاذر إن للأعمال](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%B1-%D8%A5%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84/id1208123256)
- Gathern's official X account describes a guest remedy: a problem reported within the first hour was compensated with 20% of the booking value. This is a refund policy, not a fee. — [Gathern on X](https://x.com/gathernApp/status/1604435847239110656?lang=en)
- **Saudi venue and wedding-hall apps exist, but none publish fees:**
  - Saudi Venues calls itself a "marketplace for hotels, ballrooms, and event venues". — [saudivenues.io](https://saudivenues.io/)
  - Ahjjz is a Saudi platform for wedding halls and event services. — [ahjjz.com](https://ahjjz.com/en)
  - The "Monasba" iOS app aggregates wedding halls and venues with in-app payment. — [App Store: Monasba](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/monasba/id6755484716)

It started as invitation management, has supported about 250 events in 60+ cities, and launched a B2B platform in August 2026. ### Inferences
- In the Saudi short-stay and istiraha segment (Gathern), the effective take is plausibly in the high teens to 20s%, but this is not confirmed.

### Gaps

- No data was found on "Mawsool" or Bayut-style event marketplaces.
- Kafiil's commission was not checked.

## Q2. Global comparisons

### Takeaway
Global booking marketplaces take about 15-20% in total:
- **Airbnb:** a 15.5% host-only fee. Under the older split model, hosts paid 3% and guests about 14-16.5%.
- **Booking.com:** about 15% base, with an effective 18-20%+ when payment fees and preferred programs are added.
- **The Knot and WeddingWire:** they use vendor subscriptions (roughly $100-$1,200+/month on 12-month contracts), not commissions.

### Cited Findings
- **Airbnb host-only fee:** 15.5% of the booking subtotal for most hosts. Airbnb's help pages say the fee is "usually 14%-16%", and hosts with very strict cancellation policies pay an extra 2%. — [Airbnb resource (AR)](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/how-much-does-airbnb-charge-hosts-288); [Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/airbnb-host-fees/)
- **Airbnb split model:** about 3% host plus about 14.1-16.5% guest. Sources conflict on when host-only became universal (dates cited: 13 Apr 2025, 27 Oct 2025, and 1 Dec 2025). — [Hostfully 25 Sep 2025](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/airbnb-simplified-pricing/); [Rental Scale-Up](https://www.rentalscaleup.com/airbnb-host-fees/)
- **Booking.com:** base commission about 15% (range 10-25%), plus about 1-3% payment-processing fees in some cases. Preferred-program members see an effective 18-20%+. These are secondary sources. — [Guesty](https://www.guesty.com/blog/how-much-does-booking-com-charge-hosts); [Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/simplified-pricing-airbnb-vrbo-booking-com-single-fee-structures-for-vacation-rental-managers/)
- **The Knot and WeddingWire vendor pricing (all estimates; neither publishes prices):**
  - The Knot listings: about $50-150/month in rural areas and $500-1,200+/month in major metros, on 12-month contracts with no early cancellation. — [planning.wedding (competitor)](https://planning.wedding/is-the-knot-worth-it-for-vendors)
  - Packages range from "a few hundred to several thousand dollars per month". — [Curate blog 2024](https://curate.co/blog/the-knot-advertising-cost/)
  - Standard profiles start around $120/month. — [Grade.us](https://blog.grade.us/the-knot-reviews/)
  - WeddingWire: about $125-1,000+/month.
  - Note: vendors filed a lawsuit in March 2025, and an FTC inquiry into The Knot Worldwide was reported in 2026. — [Wikipedia: The Knot Worldwide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Knot_Worldwide)
- **Wedding planner fees (US and global):** commonly 10-20% of the total wedding budget, or 10-15% for full service. Flat fees and day-of coordination packages are also common. — [Zola](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/how-much-do-wedding-coordinators-cost); [Loverly](https://loverly.com/planning/wedding-101/wedding-planner-cost); [Bridebook 2025](https://app.bridebook.com/za-en/article/how-much-does-a-wedding-planner-cost-the-2025-average-revealed)
- **Travel-agency agent model under Saudi VAT:** a disclosed agent that passes the principal's cost through without markup charges VAT only on its commission or service fee. This is a secondary source. — [Traacs ZATCA travel checklist 2026](https://www.traacs.in/zatca-e-invoicing-for-saudi-travel-agencies/)

### Inferences
- A fixed 15% markup is realistic and competitive. It sits at the low-to-middle of planner norms (10-20%), matches Booking.com's base and Airbnb's 15.5%, and is below Khamsat and Mostaql's ~20%.
- Because مناسبتي is admin-mediated (quote sourcing, negotiation, coordination), it delivers more service than a self-serve marketplace, which supports 15% or even more.
- Risks and refinements to a flat 15%:
  - **High-ticket items:** on venues of SAR 50K+, a flat 15% is large in riyal terms. Clients comparing direct hall prices may notice it, so consider tiered rates (for example, a lower % on halls and a higher % on small vendors) or a cap.
  - **Low-ticket items:** on small bookings, 15% may not cover handling, so a minimum fee may be needed.
  - **Payment fees:** gateway costs of about 1-2.5% (Q4) come out of the margin, leaving roughly 12.5-14% gross before VAT effects (Q3).

### Gaps
- No published Saudi wedding planner fee survey was found. Saudi planner pricing (percentage vs. package) remains unconfirmed; collect quotes from Riyadh and Jeddah planners on Instagram.

## Q3. Legal and tax setup in Saudi Arabia (CR vs. freelance document, e-commerce law, Maroof/SBC, VAT, e-invoicing)

### Takeaway
- **Legal vehicle:** a brokerage or reselling platform that collects client money should operate under a commercial registration (CR), not a freelance document. The e-commerce law lets non-CR "practitioners" sell online, but the freelance document covers listed professions for an individual. It is free, valid one year, and limited to 5 documents.
- **E-commerce law:** requires identity, CR and tax-number disclosure, with fines up to SAR 1M.
- **VAT:** registration is mandatory above SAR 375K of taxable supplies per 12 months and voluntary above SAR 187.5K.
- **Fatoora e-invoicing Phase 2:** has reached Wave 25 (revenue > SAR 187.5K in any year 2022-2025, deadline 1 Feb 2027).
- **VAT structure:** a reseller that buys and resells in its own name owes VAT on the full price. A disclosed agent owes VAT on its commission only.

### Cited Findings
- **E-Commerce Law definitions (Royal Decree M/126, official MISA English translation):**
  - A "merchant" is a person registered in the Commercial Register who practises e-commerce.
  - A "practitioner" is someone who practises e-commerce without that registration.
  - Both are service providers. — [MISA: E-Commerce Law PDF](https://misa.gov.sa/app/uploads/2025/07/E-Commerce-Law.pdf)
- **Disclosure duties:** the site must show the provider's full name, address, contact details, CR number, and tax registration number (if registered). CR-holding entities must register their e-commerce site with the Ministry of Commerce. — [Al Tamimi, July 2020](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/july-2020/articles/theres-something-in-your-cart-an-update-on-e-commerce-in-saudi-arabia/)
- **Penalties:** range from a warning to fines up to SAR 1,000,000, plus temporary or permanent suspension or blocking. Data breaches must be reported to the Ministry of Commerce within 3 days. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/business/retail/saudi-arabia-tightens-up-ecommerce-law-with-stiff-penalties-1.73327592); [Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/clyde/media/fileslibrary/J470323_KSA_E-commerce_Law_Interactive_-_Update_0919_FV2.pdf); [GlobalComplianceNews](https://www.globalcompliancenews.com/2020/03/16/saudi-arabia-regulates-e-commerce/)
- **Non-CR selling:** Salla's merchant guide says a person without a CR may practise e-commerce only after obtaining a freelance certificate. This is a vendor source. — [Salla help](https://help.salla.sa/en/article/merchant-ecommerce-obligations/g0cbvwqncrfmptexs580y6f7)
- **Maroof:** a 2026 compliance blog still lists Maroof registration, VAT-inclusive pricing, a written return policy, and data protection as requirements. Not verified against the official text. Maroof has reportedly been folded into the Saudi Business Center (SBC) e-commerce authentication, so verify current status on sbc.gov.sa. — [origami.sa 2026](https://origami.sa/en/blog/saudi-ecommerce-law-2026-maroof-compliance/)
- **Freelance document (وثيقة العمل الحر):** all sources are secondary.
  - It is issued free via freelance.sa (issuance, renewal and cancellation carry no fees), and is valid one year.
  - Limited to a maximum of 5 documents in different professions.
  - Eligibility: Saudi national, 18+, with Nafath/Absher access, choosing from the approved list of professions. — [khaleejcalculators](https://khaleejcalculators.com/freelance/freelance-document-eligibility-fees); [taarraf 2026](https://taarraf.com/freelance-document-saudi/); [Bayut blog](https://www.bayut.sa/blog/%D9%85%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D9%88%D8%AB%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%B1/)
  - One blog says CR issuance plus chamber membership starts from about SAR 200/year. Unverified. — [khaleejcalculators](https://khaleejcalculators.com/freelance/freelance-document-eligibility-fees)
- **VAT thresholds:** mandatory registration above SAR 375,000 of taxable supplies in 12 months; voluntary above SAR 187,500 of supplies or expenses. — [Tasis Partners](https://www.tasispartners.com/en/vat-registration-saudi-arabia); [Expandway](https://expandway.sa/vat-in-saudi-arabia/)
- **E-invoicing (Fatoora) Phase 2:** secondary sources.
  - Wave 24: taxpayers with VAT-able revenue above SAR 375K in 2022, 2023 or 2024 had to integrate by 30 June 2026.
  - Wave 25 (announced 24 July 2026): revenue above SAR 187,500 in any year from 2022 to 2025; integration deadline 1 Feb 2027.
  - No Wave 26 had been announced as of September 2026. — [beancount.io, Oct 2026](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/10/06/saudi-arabia-zatca-wave-24-e-invoicing-sme-guide); [origami.sa Wave 25](https://origami.sa/en/blog/zatca-wave-25-e-invoicing-integration-guide/)
  - Phase 1 (generating e-invoices) applies to all VAT-registered persons since Dec 2021. This is background knowledge; verify at zatca.gov.sa.
- **Agent vs. principal:**
  - ZATCA's agent guidelines distinguish a disclosed agent (acts in the principal's name) from an undisclosed agent or commissionaire (acts in its own name). The undisclosed agent is treated as making the supply itself, so it accounts for VAT on the full value. — [ZATCA Guideline for Agents (2nd version)](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Guideline-For-Agents-under-VAT-Provisions.pdf); [ZATCA Agents Guideline v1](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/Agents%20Guideline.pdf)
  - A platform's status depends on the buyer and seller relationship and the terms of sale. — [PwC Saudi e-commerce VAT guide, Sep 2021](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/tax/documents/2021/saudi-arabia-ecommerce-vat-guide-sep-2021.pdf); [ZATCA VAT Guideline for Electronic Contracts, Sep 2022](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/VAT-Guideline-for-Electronic-Contracts.pdf)
- **Deemed-supplier rules for electronic marketplaces (2025 amendments):**
  - A platform facilitating supplies by non-VAT-registered resident suppliers, or by non-residents, is treated as the deemed supplier and is responsible for the full VAT.
  - Where it is the deemed supplier, no separate VAT is charged on its commission to the supplier.
  - Where the supplier is a VAT-registered resident, the platform is not the deemed supplier.
  - These are advisory summaries; the full text was not retrieved. — [Dhruva Consultants tax alert, 30 Dec 2025](https://dhruvaconsultants.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ZATCA-Guidance-on-Deemed-Supplier-Obligations-for-Electronic-Marketplaces.pdf); [Grant Thornton SA (2026)](https://www.grantthornton.sa/en/insights/articles-and-publications/vat_and_electronic_marketplace_in_saudi_arabia/)

### Inferences
- **Reseller model and VAT:** مناسبتي's "admin gets quotes and resells with markup" model is a principal (reseller) or undisclosed-agent model. Once registered, it charges 15% VAT on the full client price, not just the 15% markup. It can recover input VAT only from VAT-registered vendors. Many small vendors (istiraha owners, home-based coordinators) are not registered, so it gets no input credit on their costs. Effective VAT then falls on the whole value, which can wipe out most of a 15% margin unless prices are set VAT-inclusive with that in mind.
- **Agent alternative:** restructure as a disclosed agent. Invoices go in the vendor's name, and مناسبتي invoices a commission or service fee. VAT then applies only to the fee. This needs contracts and invoicing that clearly show the vendor as supplier.
- **Deemed-supplier caveat:** if مناسبتي facilitates unregistered vendors through an electronic platform, it may be pulled into deemed-supplier status anyway. Get advice from a Saudi tax adviser.
- **VAT threshold impact:** under the reseller model, gross bookings (not just the margin) count toward the SAR 375K threshold. That threshold is roughly 25-40 weddings. Under the agent model only commissions count, so registration would come much later.
- **Legal vehicle:** a CR (e.g. sole establishment or LLC) with activities such as event organising/management and e-commerce or brokerage is the safer vehicle. Payment gateways also typically require a CR (see Q4).

### Gaps
- The exact ISIC activity codes for event-booking brokerage on a CR were not confirmed. Check the Ministry of Commerce or SBC activity list.
- The current Maroof/SBC authentication requirement was not confirmed from an official page.
- The full ZATCA deemed-supplier text (regulation article, effective date) was not retrieved, because the Grant Thornton page was blocked by this session's network.
- Whether the freelance document's profession list includes "event coordination" was not verified.

## Q4. Payment gateways in Saudi Arabia (fees, settlement, marketplace splits)

### Takeaway
Most Saudi gateways quote per merchant. Indicative online pricing in 2025-2026:
- **mada:** about 1-1.75% (often + SAR 1).
- **Visa/Mastercard:** about 2.25-2.85% + SAR 0.3-1.
- **Setup and monthly fees:** vary from zero to about SAR 999 setup plus SAR 199/month.
- **Settlement:** about T+1 to T+5.

Moyasar, Tap and PayTabs advertise marketplace split payouts. All require a CR or freelance document and separate marketplace approval.

### Cited Findings
- **Moyasar:**
  - Its own FAQ and site say pricing comes from sales, based on volume.
  - Third-party estimates: "from 2.5%", or about 2.5-2.9% + about SAR 1.
  - Supports mada, Visa/MC/Amex, STC Pay, Apple Pay, and Samsung Pay (subject to approval).
  - Marketplace page: split payments at checkout (platform commission, merchant share, partners) with per-merchant settlement statements. — [Moyasar FAQ](https://www.moyasar.com/en/resources/faqs); [Moyasar marketplaces](https://moyasar.com/en/solutions/platforms-and-marketplaces/); [Moyasar startups & freelancers](https://moyasar.com/en/solutions/startups-and-freelancers/); [paymentproviders.io](https://paymentproviders.io/compare/moyasar-vs-alipay?focus=fees); [NOWPayments (competitor blog)](https://nowpayments.io/blog/payment-gateway-saudi-arabia)
- **Tap Payments:**
  - One review lists Saudi online mada at 1% and local Visa/MC at 2.5%, plus SAR 1 per transaction. It also lists up to SAR 600/month or up to SAR 5,000 setup, notes these are maximums, and says pricing is customised by volume. — [paymentreview.com](https://paymentreview.com/reviews/tap-payments/)
  - Other comparisons cite about 2.85% + SAR 0.30. — [logiolegion 2026](https://logiolegion.com/blogs/tap-payments-vs-hyperpay-vs-moyasar-saudi-arabia-2026)
  - Stores on the Matajer platform (via Tap) pay mada 1.75% + SAR 1 and cards 2.75% + SAR 1, with no setup fee. — [Tap blog on mada fees](https://blog.tap.company/new-mada-fees-saudi/)
  - Settlement is about T+2 to T+3.
  - Tap's marketplace product splits funds to providers and keeps the platform's commission. It reportedly needs separate approval, and retailer and marketplace must be in the same country. — [Tap marketplaces](https://www.tap.company/en-sa/products/marketplaces)
- **HyperPay:**
  - mada reported at 1.5% + SAR 1; cards about 2.5% + SAR 1 (negotiable).
  - HyperPay itself says fees are set in a merchant-terms annex.
  - Settlement is about T+1 to T+3. — [HyperPay blog 2026](https://www.hyperpay.com/blog/best-payment-gateway-in-saudi-arabia/); [raghdan.sa 2025 guide](https://raghdan.sa/en/news/payment-gateways-in-saudi-arabia-guide-2025-requirements-fees-registration-steps-and-comprehensive-comparison/)
- **PayTabs:**
  - One published figure: SAR 49/month app, mada 1%, cards 2.25% + SAR 1, marked "in testing, subject to change".
  - Another published agreement: SAR 999 setup, SAR 199/month, and SAR 1 per transaction.
  - Supports Split Payout to multiple beneficiaries, which requires deposit and 2FA setup.
  - Settlement estimated at T+5 to about a week. — [paymentproviders.io UAE & KSA 2026](https://paymentproviders.io/blog/best-payment-gateways-uae-saudi-arabia); [cs-cart](https://www.cs-cart.com/blog/payment-gateways-in-saudi-arabia/)
- **Geidea:** no public online rate card was found ("standard market rates aligned with mada regulations"). — [photonpay 2026 guide](https://www.photonpay.com/hk/blog/article/payment-gateway-in-saudi-arabia)
- **mada price cap:** the regulated mada cap (0.8%, max SAR 40) applies to in-store POS, not online gateways. — [paymentproviders.io](https://paymentproviders.io/blog/best-payment-gateways-uae-saudi-arabia)
- **Regulatory caution:** marketplace fund holding, split payouts, and stored value may require Saudi legal and regulatory advice (SAMA payment-services rules) before launch. — [paymentproviders.io](https://paymentproviders.io/blog/best-payment-gateways-uae-saudi-arabia)

### Inferences
- **Blended fee:** with mostly mada payers, expect about 1.2-2% blended gateway cost on a SAR 30K wedding package (SAR 360-600).
- **Reseller model:** collecting the full amount and paying vendors yourself avoids needing split payouts. The cost is holding client funds, which brings bank-transfer overhead and possible regulatory questions.
- **Agent model:** a gateway split (Moyasar or Tap marketplace) fits better, sending the vendor share directly.
- **Large payments:** for very large venue deposits, bank transfer (SARIE/instant) may be cheaper than card payment.

### Gaps
- No official, current public rate cards were found for Moyasar, HyperPay or Geidea. Get written quotes.
- STC Pay and Apple Pay surcharges were not separately confirmed; they are usually priced like cards or mada depending on the underlying card.
- Exact onboarding documents were not confirmed: CR or freelance document, IBAN in the entity's name, national address, website with policies.

## Q5. Mawthooq (موثوق) influencer advertising license

### Takeaway
The Mawthooq license costs SAR 15,000 and is valid for 3 years. It is required for Saudi individuals who publish paid ads on social media, and is issued by the General Authority for Media Regulation (GAMR, formerly GCAM). مناسبتي does not need it to run its own ads, but should hire licensed influencers or agencies, or verify their license.

### Cited Findings
- The fee is SAR 15,000 for a 3-year license, applied for via GCAM/GAMR. It covers X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat and Facebook. — [Arab News: GCAM lists Mawthooq rules](https://arabnews.com/node/2176661); [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/home/article/3793846/saudi-arabia-requires-social-media-influencers-obtain-license-publish-ad?page=2); [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/what-is-the-mawthooq-license)
- Non-Saudi influencers need an investment license, then a trade license and a media license, then Mawthooq. Non-Saudi residents must otherwise be represented by licensed advertising agencies. — [Clyde & Co, Sept 2022](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2022/09/new-social-media-requirements-for-saudi-arabia)
- One single secondary source lists eligibility as age 18+, a clean record, and six months of compliant content. Penalties of "up to SAR 5 million" for unlicensed ads also come from PR or marketing sites. Both are unverified. — [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/the-rise-of-influencer-marketing-in-saudi-arabia-opportunities-challenges-and-key-players); [communicateonline.me](https://communicateonline.me/news/influencer-marketing-by-brand-ripplr-the-latest-updates-on-influencer-legislations-and-their-impact-on-businesses/)

### Inferences
- For marketing, budget for influencers who hold Mawthooq, or contract through a licensed agency. Ask for the license number before paying, since the advertiser may share liability.
- The founders would need their own Mawthooq only if they personally publish paid ads for third parties as influencers.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 official confirmation of the fee was found (no change was reported); check mawthooq.sa or gmedia.gov.sa.
- The exact penalty schedule was not confirmed from an official source.
