# Competitive Landscape for "مناسبتي" (Saudi Arabia, Eastern Province focus, 2024-2026)

Method note (read first): research done 2026-11-09. WebFetch was blocked in this session for most Saudi domains (mazoom.sa, kart.sa, zafaf.sa, enterpriseam.com returned DNS/allowlist errors), so the findings below come from search-engine snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not full page reads. Prices and feature claims are almost all self-reported on vendor websites (marketing). They are marked "confirmed (self-reported)" when a vendor page states them, and "estimate/inference" otherwise. Several pages showed internally inconsistent prices (notably Mazoom); these conflicts are flagged.

## 1. Wedding hall / venue booking platforms in KSA — who covers the Eastern Province?

### Takeaway
The venue layer is fragmented among small directories/booking apps (Zafaf.net, عالم زفاف zafaf.sa, Ahjjz, فرحة frhsa, Wedgizer, أفراح apps) — none appears to have dominant scale or disclosed funding. Eastern Province (Dammam/Khobar/Ahsa) coverage exists on Zafaf.net (~29 venues listed for الشرقية), فرحة (Khobar halls, Ahsa catering pages), Wedgizer and Arabia Weddings, but no platform shows deep, transactional EP inventory. Gathern is the big funded player but for istirahas/chalets, not halls.

### Cited Findings
- **Zafaf.net (زفاف.نت, Saudi section)**: directory of halls, hotel halls, istirahas and vendors; has dedicated Dammam pages (venues, private halls, "Dammam weddings" vendor page) and describes itself as the largest directory of wedding halls in Dammam, with Khobar hotel halls and Ahsa venues; Eastern Province listing shows ~29 venues. Most vendor prices are "اطلب السعر" (request a price). — [Zafaf.net Dammam venues](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-venues/dammam); [Zafaf.net الشرقية](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-venues/ashsharqiah); [Zafaf.net Dammam weddings](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/dammam-weddings)
- Zafaf.net offers a free concierge-like matching: client chooses budget and desired features and their team sends suitable halls for free (closest existing analogue to مناسبتي's budget→venue suggestion). — [Zafaf.net private venues](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues) (as summarized by search)
- Zafaf.net price examples for Dammam: Al-Jawsaq Al-Sharqi halls from SAR 73,000 for both sections. — [Zafaf.net Dammam](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-venues/dammam)

### Inferences
- No venue platform has demonstrated dominant traction; most are directories/SEO sites with "request price" flows — validating مناسبتي's "no public prices, admin-collected quotes" approach as market-normal, but also meaning it is not by itself a differentiator.
- Gathern is an indirect competitor/channel: many EP families use istirahas booked via Gathern for smaller/family events; مناسبتي could partner rather than compete.
- Transactional deposit-based marketplaces (فرحة, Ahjjz) are the closest venue-layer threat; فرحة already has Khobar/Ahsa pages.

### Gaps
- Could not verify Saudi Venues, Monasba/مناسبة app, or a Saudi "Joy" app (the "Joy" found is the US withjoy.com wedding site, reviewed at [theprivateweddingapp.com](https://www.theprivateweddingapp.com/blog/joy-wedding-invitations-review-2026)). No download counts or EP venue counts for Ahjjz/فرحة/أفراح were found. No funding found for any hall-booking app.

## 2. Event vendor marketplaces, wedding planning apps, and full-service planners (Dammam/Khobar)

### Takeaway
Vendor discovery is dominated by directories (Arabia Weddings ~2,650 Saudi vendors; Zafaf.net) and informal channels (Instagram, Haraj, WhatsApp). "Request-for-quote" bundles exist (SaudiWedding.com, Arabia Weddings free quotes). Full-service planners in Dammam exist as small agencies; published fee guidance is SAR 2k–30k for coordination, with luxury firms far higher.

### Cited Findings
- **Arabia Weddings**: ~2,648 vendors across 21 Saudi cities (Riyadh 1,138, Jeddah 706); 15,447 vendors / 151 cities / 30 countries overall. Free, no-commitment quote requests; quotes arrive by email or WhatsApp. Dammam planners listed include Anti Ghayr, 4th Ave Events, Marriagement Events Planner, November Event, hart2hart event, Nawaim Wedding Planner. No traffic data found. — [Arabia Weddings KSA](https://www.arabiaweddings.com/saudi-arabia); [Arabia Weddings home](https://www.arabiaweddings.com/); [Dammam planners](https://www.arabiaweddings.com/dammam/vendors/planners)
- **SaudiWedding (saudi-wedding.com)**: one request for hall + kosha + photography + catering etc. instead of contacting each; quote requests free; client free to accept/reject; agreement is made directly with the provider (i.e., lead-gen, not managed). — [saudi-wedding.com](https://saudi-wedding.com/)
- **Haraj** as informal channel: e.g., "تنسيق حفلات الدمام والخبر" ad with prices starting ~SAR 350 / 700 (unit/scope unclear). — [Haraj ad](https://haraj.com.sa/en/11162708129/%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%82_%D8%AD%D9%81%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85_%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%B1/)
- Planner fee guidance: basic coordination SAR 2,000–10,000; mid plans with supervision/vendor coordination SAR 15,000–30,000 (self-published). — [zafaf.sa planners](https://zafaf.sa/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%85%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%81%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA/)
- Luxury end: Saudi Event Management lists Elite tier from SAR 250,000 and Royal tier from SAR 500,000 (includes VIP guest management). — [saudieventmanagement.com](https://saudieventmanagement.com/services/royal-weddings)
- Wezoree also lists Saudi wedding vendors (directory). — [Wezoree](https://wezoree.com/vendors/saudi-arabia-area/)
- Budget tooling exists free: ehsabi.com Saudi wedding budget calculator (6 categories + 10% reserve; also mentions MHRSD "دعم الزواج" support of SAR 60,000 — needs official verification). — [ehsabi.com](https://ehsabi.com/c/wedding-budget-ksa/)
- Global-style planning app marketed in Arabic: The Private Wedding App — invitation, RSVP, guest list, seating, budget, checklist, vendor comparison, AI planner; $19.99/month or $189 lifetime (self-reported; its blog ranks itself #1). Its Arabic blog describes scanning barcodes at the women's and men's hall doors from a supervisor's phone with per-hall counts. — [theprivateweddingapp.com](https://www.theprivateweddingapp.com/); [Arabic barcode blog](https://www.theprivateweddingapp.com/ar/blog/wedding-invitation-barcode-entry-pass)

### Inferences
- The "budget + taste → curated venue+vendor bundle with real quotes" offering is partially present (Zafaf.net free hall matching; SaudiWedding multi-service RFQ; Arabia Weddings quotes), but these are lead-gen handoffs — the client still negotiates and coordinates alone. مناسبتي's admin-mediated quote collection is closer to a light "planner" service at platform cost.
- Real competition for EP clients likely remains Instagram/WhatsApp-based coordinators (نسقات/منظمات) and family networks; they are cheap (hundreds to low thousands SAR on Haraj) and trusted via referrals.

### Gaps
- INVE (Riyadh) found, but "Fadfada", "Mazaya" as wedding marketplaces not found. No fee data from specific Dammam/Khobar agencies (Arabia Weddings listings don't show prices). Instagram planner follower counts/fees not retrievable without fetch access.

## 3. Digital invitations + RSVP + per-guest QR door check-in services in KSA

### Takeaway
This layer is crowded and price-competitive: at least 10+ Saudi providers (Mazoom/معزوم, Kart/كرت, Mshala/مسهلا, Asfrt/اسفرت, Creator InvitePass, Wethaq/وثاق, Mira's/مِراس, MyInvite, dinvite, دعوتي, لمسة, أنورات, مرحبا, Munasbat). Typical pricing: free tier up to ~100 guests or 1 invitation; ~SAR 125–380 per 25–100 invites on per-invite plans; flat SAR 149–449 per event on SaaS plans; staffed door check-in as an add-on (~SAR 500 per 250 guests at Mazoom). Men/women door separation with gendered supervisors is already offered (MyInvite, Private Wedding App).

### Cited Findings
- **Mazoom (معزوم)**: per-guest entry QR, RSVP; QR delivered via WhatsApp after guest confirms; app for managing and scanning; free plan up to 100 guests (QR only on paid); door-staff service SAR 500 per 250 guests (included by default in some packages). Prices conflict across its own pages: "Plus" SAR 199/event up to 500 guests and "Pro" SAR 499/month (rsvp.mazoom.sa); SAR 299 per 100 invites vs SAR 1,200 per 100 in "managed/premium" (blog.mazoom.sa); premium from SAR 850 for 50 guests (landing). Also has a partners program and free invitation designer. — [mazoom.sa](https://www.mazoom.sa/); [rsvp.mazoom.sa](https://rsvp.mazoom.sa/); [blog.mazoom.sa/managed](https://blog.mazoom.sa/managed); [QR check-in blog](https://landing.mazoom.sa/blog/qr-check-in-event-day); [partners](https://landing.mazoom.sa/partners); [designer](https://design.mazoom.sa/)
- **Kart (كرت)**: named invitations via WhatsApp/SMS, live replies, per-guest entry barcode after RSVP, Apple Wallet pass, scanned from supervisor's phone; packages by reply/barcode options (prices not retrieved). — [kart.sa](https://www.kart.sa/)
- **Mshala (مسهلا)**: free 1 invite; 10 = SAR 37.99; 25 = 94.99; 50 = 189.99; 100 = 379.99; unlimited = 1,099.99; QR per guest; WhatsApp sending. — [mshala.sa](https://mshala.sa/)
- **Asfrt (اسفرت)**: SAR 125 for 25 invitations; WhatsApp sending, RSVP, per-person code; scanning app on any smart device. — [asfrt.com](https://asfrt.com/)
- **Creator (InvitePass)**: free up to 100 guests; Basic SAR 199 (≤500 guests), Pro SAR 449 (≤2,500 guests), one-time; door scanning with duplicate-entry prevention. — [creator-invitepass.com](https://creator-invitepass.com/)

- **MyInvite (ماي انفايت)**: on event day, male and female supervisors scan barcodes for men's and women's sections; end-of-day attendance report. — [myinvite.sa](https://myinvite.sa/ar/%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A9-%D8%B2%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/p598053621)

- INVE is the only invitation player with disclosed VC funding, but is pivoting to B2B/corporate events — less focus on EP weddings.
- No provider found explicitly advertising VIP arrival alerts to the host/family or a live coordination chat room; that remains a potential differentiator (unverified absence).

## 5. Gaps in the market — does anyone combine everything? Closest end-to-end competitor

### Takeaway
No single competitor found combines budget-based venue+vendor matching, admin-mediated real quotes, invitations with per-guest QR, gendered door reception with VIP alerts, a live event-night coordination room, family task/budget tools, and post-event thank-you messages. The market splits into (a) venue/vendor discovery & booking (Zafaf.net, فرحة, Ahjjz, Arabia Weddings, SaudiWedding) and (b) guest-management/invitation tools (Mazoom, Kart, Meras, Wethaq, MyInvite, INVE). Closest partial competitors: **Mazoom** (invitations + QR + staffed door check-in, partners program) on the event-night side; **Zafaf.net / SaudiWedding** on the budget-matching/quotes side; **فرحة** as a deposit-based transactional vendor marketplace with Khobar/Ahsa pages; **The Private Wedding App** on the planning-tool side (budget, checklist, vendor comparison, gendered door scanning) but without local vendor sourcing.

### Cited Findings
- Zafaf.net: budget + features → team sends matching halls free. — [Zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues)
- SaudiWedding: one request for hall/kosha/photography/catering, free quotes, deal direct with vendor. — [saudi-wedding.com](https://saudi-wedding.com/)
- Mazoom: QR entry + RSVP + staffed check-in (SAR 500/250 guests). — [Mazoom blog](https://landing.mazoom.sa/blog/qr-check-in-event-day)
