# Eastern Province local wedding-services competition facing "مناسبتي" (2025-2026)

> Method note (2026-11-09): Research ran through web search only. In this session WebFetch could not reach outside hosts (busharh.com, mazoom.sa, hayyacom.com and merasapp.com all failed DNS), so **every figure below comes from search-engine snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the pages in full**. Treat prices as "reported on the vendor's or directory's page, date unverified" unless a date is given. Many directory pages (zafaf.sa, zafaf.net) were about 300-450 days old when indexed, which puts them roughly in mid-to-late 2024 or 2025. All amounts are in SAR unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Full-service wedding planners and coordination companies (Dammam / Khobar / Qatif / Jubail / Al-Ahsa)

### Takeaway
The Eastern Province has no dominant full-service "wedding planner" brand. Most local players are kosha/decor coordinators (منسقات/كوش وتنسيق) working from Instagram, Haraj or zafaf.net listings. They price by bundled decor package, not by flat planning fee or % of budget. The one published starting price runs from about SAR 350 (cheap Haraj coordinator) up to SAR 25,000 (Khobar kosha/hall-styling firm).

### Cited Findings
- **Mais Events Planner (ميس لتنسيق الحفلات), Khobar**: kosha, aisle, curtains, guest tables and favors. Listing says prices **start from SAR 25,000** — [zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-planning/alkhobar/mais-events-planner-khobar-43966)
- **Haidy Kssaby (هايدي كسابي لتنسيق الاحتفالات), Khobar**: full hall styling, including the kosha and flower-decorated gift tables. No price shown — [zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-planning/alkhobar/haidy-kssaby-event-planner-khobar-45315)
- **SIA (سيا لتنظيم الحفلات), Khobar**: kosha plus event organisation with "a specialised supervision team" (فريق إشراف). This is the closest local analogue to night-of coordination — [zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-planning/alkhobar/sia-wedding-planning-org-khobar-29838)
- **Elite Al-Nukhba (إيليت النخبة), Qatif**: decor design, kosha, flowers and table styling — [zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-planning/alquatif/elite-weddings-flowers-36914)
- zafaf.net keeps a curated "Eastern Province wedding planners 2025" gallery, so the directory is a live acquisition channel for these firms — [zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-planning/galleries/wedding-planners-in-asharqiyah-308)
- **WS Events planning (@ws_events_20)** on Instagram has about 24k followers and advertises in Qatif, Dammam and Khobar. This is the largest Instagram-native local coordinator found — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ws_events_20/)
- **@jubailevent (حفلات الجبيل)** is a Jubail event/party coordinator with only 15 followers and 5 posts, which shows how fragmented and tiny many accounts are — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/jubailevent/)
- **Al-Ibdaa Al-Mumayyaz (الإبداع المميز للحفلات)**: tents, chairs, tables, kosha and flowers across Dammam, Khobar, Qatif, Dhahran, Abqaiq and Al-Ahsa (plus Riyadh) — [alibdaevents.com](https://alibdaevents.com/)
- **Al-Kadi (الكادي تنظيم وإدارة الفعاليات)**, Dammam/Eastern Province: tents, screens, sound and lighting, **guest-entry management** (إدارة دخول الضيوف), coffee service, chair and table rental. Found as a Mourjan classified — [mourjan.com](https://www.mourjan.com/sa/dammam/events-planning/2/)
- A Haraj ad for "تنسيق حفلات الدمام والخبر" says prices "start from 350 depending on choice". The ad is undated and does not say what is included — [haraj.com.sa](https://haraj.com.sa/en/11162708129/%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%82_%D8%AD%D9%81%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85_%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%B1/)
- **Al Jazeera International** says it does wedding planning and coordination, with an office in Dammam, serving Dammam and Khobar (page about 300 days old) — [aljazeeraint.com](https://aljazeeraint.com/wedding-event-planning-in-saudi-arabia/)
- Small Khobar planners on Facebook: **Rinad Wedding Planner** (about 128 likes) and **Ghali Arts Wedding Planner** (about 498 likes) — [Facebook/Rinad](https://www.facebook.com/RinadSiddiqui/), [Facebook/Ghali Arts](https://www.facebook.com/GhaliArtsWeddingPlanner/)
- National or out-of-region players also claim the area. **Jedweddings** (Jeddah-based) lists Jeddah, Riyadh and Dammam as service cities. **Saudi Event Management** (Riyadh/Jeddah/AlUla) runs a Khobar landing page. **Eventia** is a general event company with an Eastern Province page — [Instagram/jedweddings](https://www.instagram.com/jedweddings/), [saudieventmanagement.com](https://saudieventmanagement.com/locations/khobar), [eventiaevents.com](https://www.eventiaevents.com/locations/event-management-company-in-al-khobar/)

### Inferences
- In practice, "planner" in the Eastern Province means a decor and kosha vendor. Budget-matching, quote comparison and guest logistics across several vendors are not offered as a product by anyone found. The bride's family (often the mother or sisters) does that integration work.
- Fee model: everything found is package or quote-based, priced as a decor bundle. No local planner was found publishing a % of budget or a separate planning or coordination fee. This is an inference from the absence of such pricing, not a confirmed market norm.
- Premium decor firms (about SAR 25k+) and national luxury planners serve the top tier. A long tail of Instagram/Haraj coordinators (from SAR 350) serves the rest. A platform could sit between them by aggregating the long tail.

### Gaps
- No verified follower counts or Snapchat handles for the Khobar firms (Mais, Haidy Kssaby, SIA). Pages could not be fetched.
- No confirmed % of budget or flat planning-fee examples in the Eastern Province.
- No Al-Ahsa-specific planner brands were identified by name.

## 2. Venues selling all-in packages (hall + dinner + kosha + coffee + photography)

### Takeaway
Bundled venue packages are common in Dammam/Khobar and are a direct substitute for a multi-vendor platform. Hotels charge per head (about SAR 200-250 per chair). Large halls sell two-section packages from about SAR 15k-35k, and premium halls run up to about SAR 55k-60k. Photography is often *not* included, and some halls ban outside catering, which locks families into the hall's own buffet.

### Cited Findings
- zafaf.sa's 2025 price guide puts average hall prices in Dammam/Khobar/Dhahran at **SAR 10,000 for small halls to SAR 30,000+ for luxury halls**, with seasonal offers — [zafaf.sa](https://zafaf.sa/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AD/)
- The same guide (nationwide, not Eastern Province-specific) says inclusive food and decor packages start **about SAR 200 per person in mid-range hotels and can exceed SAR 1,000 in luxury hotels** — [zafaf.sa](https://zafaf.sa/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AD/)
- Hotel halls in Dammam/Khobar price **per chair**: **Mövenpick Khobar SAR 215-250** and **Le Méridien Khobar SAR 200-225** per person. The page was about 1,180 days old when indexed, so these are roughly 2023 prices — [zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/hotels/ideas/top-hotel-venues-in-ash-1322)

### Inferences
- Al-Ahsa halls cost about 40-60% of Dammam/Khobar halls, going by the ranges above. A budget-matching engine needs separate city price bands.
- What a package includes varies a lot from hall to hall (dinner yes/no, photography usually no, outside buffet sometimes banned). That is the price-opacity problem a platform can address with standardised side-by-side quotes.
- Halls that bundle supervisors (Al-Jawsaq, Al-Kristal) already compete with مناسبتي's event-night coordination. Halls that don't, and hotels that sell per head, are where a platform's coordination add-on fits best.

### Gaps
- No 2026-dated package price sheets. The figures are directory estimates, probably 2024-2025.
- No package data found for Jubail or Qatif halls.
- zafaf.net Al-Ahsa listings show numbers like "200-400" that are probably capacity, not price (flagged by the search summary).

## 3. Digital invitation and QR guest-entry services (KSA-wide, usable in the Eastern Province)

### Takeaway
This is the most crowded and commoditised part of the market. At least 10 Saudi services sell per-guest WhatsApp invitations with RSVP and single-use QR entry. Prices run from **free (up to 100 guests, link-only)** to about **SAR 1.40-2.60 per guest**, or bundles of about **SAR 389-719 for 50-200 guests**. None found is Eastern Province-specific. مناسبتي will not win on the invitation feature alone.

### Cited Findings
- **Mazoom (معزوم)**: free simple RSVP link up to 100 guests (no WhatsApp sending). Basic package from **about SAR 79 for ~10 guests** with automatic WhatsApp sending. Premium from **about SAR 850 for 50 guests** — [landing.mazoom.sa](https://landing.mazoom.sa/); [mazoom.sa](https://www.mazoom.sa/)
- **Busharh (بشاره)**: **50 guests SAR 389, 100 guests SAR 499, 200 guests SAR 719**, plus SAR 1 per extra guest. Each guest gets a named WhatsApp invitation with an accept/decline link — [busharh.com](https://www.busharh.com/)
- **Meras (مِراس)**: WhatsApp or email invitations with a unique QR per guest. Wedding packages from **SAR 149**, as a monthly subscription or single-event pack — [merasapp.com](https://merasapp.com/)
- **Anwarat (أنورت)**: basic **SAR 1.40 per guest** (named card plus personal QR), premium SAR 2.00 per guest, professional SAR 2.60 per guest — [anwarat.net](https://anwarat.net/)
- **Hayakm (hayakm.com, "حياكم")**: invitations "from SAR 1.50 per invitation" — [hayakm.com](https://www.hayakm.com/)
- **Hayyacom (حياكم, hayyacom.com)**: Saudi platform operating **since 2018**. WhatsApp invitation, entry QR and RSVP tracking. Price not visible. Note there are two separate "حياكم" brands (hayakm.com and hayyacom.com) — [hayyacom.com](https://hayyacom.com/ar)
- **Amsiyah (أمسية)**: basic **SAR 399 for up to 50 invitations**, extra invitations SAR 2 each. Premium from **SAR 1,399 for up to 50** — [amsiyah.sa](https://amsiyah.sa/packages.html)
- **Asfrt (اسفرت)**: **SAR 125 for 25 invitations** — [asfrt.com](https://asfrt.com/)
- **Daawty (دعوتي)**: per-guest personalisation, WhatsApp sending, live RSVP and QR entry. Price not shown — [daawty.com](https://daawty.com/)
- **Azimti (عزيمتي)**: upload the guest list as Excel; on confirmation the guest receives a personal entry QR — [azimti.app](https://azimti.app/)
- **Mazeem (معازيم)**: instant RSVP buttons, entry QR, live reports. One tier is sold *without* RSVP buttons — [mazeem.app](https://mazeem.app/)
- **da3wa.online (KSA)**: WhatsApp wedding cards with QR and RSVP, priced in SAR. Markets QR as stopping "gate-crashers" (المتطفلين) — [da3wa.online](https://da3wa.online/sa); [da3wa.online barcode page](https://da3wa.online/%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%83%D9%88%D8%AF-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B2%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81)
- Others: **kart.sa** ("each guest by name, replies arrive instantly"), **munasbatsa.com** (barcode invitations with RSVP) — [kart.sa](https://www.kart.sa/); [munasbatsa.com](https://munasbatsa.com/)
- Freelance floor price: on Khamsat, a basic e-invite design costs about **USD 5-15**, and a QR invitation bundle **USD 50 for 60 codes / USD 100 for 125 / USD 150 for 200** — [Khamsat QR offer](https://khamsat.com/designing/wedding-invitation/3794051-%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B2%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B9-qr-code); [Khamsat basic](https://khamsat.com/designing/wedding-invitation/4434509-%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B2%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%AF-%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A8-%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AD%D9%86%D8%A9)
- TikTok sellers (e.g. @qr_cood) push "single-use barcode, one entry card per female guest" and sell through WhatsApp — [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@qr_cood/video/7509181638236966162)
- A privacy point made in a market summary: shared-link invitations expose the guest list to anyone with the link, while named per-guest sending does not — [kart.sa](https://www.kart.sa/) (vendor marketing)

### Inferences
- Implied market price at 300 guests: about SAR 420-780 from per-guest sellers (Anwarat/Hayakm) or about SAR 900 from bundle sellers (Busharh: 719 plus 100 extra at SAR 1). The free tier (Mazoom) undercuts at the low end. Bundle math is an estimate.
- Women's-section privacy (separate female scanners, no photos of female guests, women-only lists) was **not** clearly advertised by any service in the snippets. This could be a gap or a positioning angle for مناسبتي (unconfirmed).
- Gate scanning seems to be either self-service (a host app) or done by the hall's supervisors. Few services appear to send their own on-site scanning staff, but this could not be verified.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Hayyacom, Daawty or Mazoom provide on-site scanning staff in Dammam/Khobar, or what they charge for it.
- No women's-section-privacy feature confirmed for any service.
- No market-share or event-volume figures for any invitation platform.

## 4. Event-night coordinators and hostess/supervisor agencies (منظمات حفلات / مشرفات قاعات)

### Takeaway
Little standalone public pricing exists. Female supervisors usually come **bundled with the hall** (e.g. Al-Kristal's four women's-section supervisors, Al-Jawsaq's package "with supervisors"). Separate night-of organisers sell through Haraj/Instagram with quote-based pricing.

### Cited Findings
- Al-Kristal (Dammam) women's section: four female supervisors, service staff, a cloakroom attendant and a hall supervisor who follows the event — [zafaf.sa](https://zafaf.sa/listing/%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%8A-%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%82%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85/)
- Al-Jawsaq bronze hall: SAR 15,000 weekday package for 100 people **with supervisors included** — [zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/al-jawsaq-wedding-halls-1103)
- SIA (Khobar) advertises a "specialised supervision team" alongside its kosha work — [zafaf.net](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/wedding-planning/alkhobar/sia-wedding-planning-org-khobar-29838)
- Al-Kadi (Dammam) lists guest-entry management as a service — [mourjan.com](https://www.mourjan.com/sa/dammam/events-planning/2/)
- Haraj Dammam/Khobar coordination ad: "from 350" (scope unclear) — [haraj.com.sa](https://haraj.com.sa/en/11162708129/%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%82_%D8%AD%D9%81%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA_%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85_%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%B1/)

### Inferences
- Hall-supplied supervisors handle the hall, not the family's guest list. A platform-run check-in tied to invitations, with a live dashboard for the family, is a different job from the hall's supervisors, and that can be positioned as complementary rather than competing.

### Gaps
- No per-night or per-hostess price for independent Eastern Province hostess agencies was found. Haraj and Instagram listings are the likely sources but could not be fetched. This needs field or WhatsApp mystery-shopping.

## 5. How these players market (Snapchat, Instagram, Haraj, WhatsApp, influencers, directories)

### Takeaway
Acquisition runs through Instagram portfolios, WhatsApp click-to-chat, Haraj/Mourjan classifieds, the zafaf.sa / zafaf.net directories (whose "best/cheapest halls in Dammam" SEO articles rank highly), and paid regional Snapchat "سناب الشرقية" ad accounts. No direct evidence was found of specific paid wedding-vendor partnerships with those Snap accounts.

### Cited Findings

## 6. Customer pain points with current options

### Takeaway
No well-documented Eastern Province complaints (X posts, forum threads) turned up in this search pass. The pain points below come from what vendors claim to fix and from structural evidence: package contents differ from hall to hall, catering is locked in, prices change by season, gate-crashers get in, and invitations fail to arrive. These are indirect evidence, not verified customer complaints.

### Cited Findings
- Price opacity, structurally: hall listings differ on whether dinner is included, whether photography is offered and whether outside buffets are allowed, and prices change on weekends and in season — [zafaf.net Al-Jawsaq](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/al-jawsaq-wedding-halls-1103); [zafaf.net Al-Rawda](https://saudi-arabia.zafaf.net/private-wedding-venues/dammam/rawda-venue); [zafaf.sa Al-Ahsa](https://zafaf.sa/%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AD-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AD%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A1/)
- Conflicting published prices for the same hall (Al-Masha'el: SAR 20k vs 25k; Al-Kristal: 10-16k vs 7-15k vs 55-60k packages) — [zafaf.sa](https://zafaf.sa/%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B6%D9%84-%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AD-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85/); [zafaf.sa Al-Kristal](https://zafaf.sa/listing/%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%8A-%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%82%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85/)

### Inferences
- The pain points a platform can most credibly address are: (1) incomparable quotes and package contents; (2) the family carrying the integration work across hall, kosha, invitation and hostesses; (3) checking that the guest list matches who actually arrives at the door. Vendor no-shows and poor coordination are plausible but **unverified** for this region.
