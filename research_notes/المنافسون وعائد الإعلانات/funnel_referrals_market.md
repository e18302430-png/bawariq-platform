# Funnel, Referral and Market-Size Benchmarks for "مناسبتي" (10,000 SAR/month social ads, Eastern Province)

Research note (2026-11-09). Network limits: several primary hosts could not be fetched from this session (stats.gov.sa, rankrushksa.com, pros.weddingpro.com, 23hublab.com, indigodigital.com), so some figures come from search-result summaries of those pages instead of the full text. Each figure is marked **[confirmed]** (official or large-sample first-party data), **[vendor estimate]** (agency or platform blog with no stated method) or **[inference]** (my own calculation). FX: 1 USD = 3.75 SAR.

## 1. Paid social cost benchmarks in KSA (Snapchat, TikTok, Instagram/Meta)

### Takeaway
Saudi agency blogs put CPMs for the main formats at about 14-22 SAR, CPC at about 0.75-3 SAR, and form-lead CPL at about 16-90 SAR for mid-market categories. A qualified lead costs roughly 3x the raw form lead. None of these numbers is audited, and Snap publishes no rate card, so a 10k SAR budget needs a 2-4 week test before the model is trusted.

### Cited Findings
- **Snapchat / Meta / TikTok CPM in KSA:** The core formats (Snap Ads, Meta Feed, TikTok In-Feed) cluster around **14-16 SAR CPM**. Stories/Reels run **18-22 SAR CPM** **[vendor estimate, Riyadh agency]** — [RankRush KSA benchmarks](https://rankrushksa.com/blog/social-media/snapchat-meta-tiktok-ksa-benchmarks/) (from a search summary; the page itself could not be fetched).
- Another agency guide puts Snap Story Ads at ~18 SAR CPM and Collection Ads at ~22 SAR CPM **[vendor estimate]** — [RankRush Snapchat Saudi 2026](https://rankrushksa-2tk.pages.dev/blog/social-media/snapchat-saudi-arabia-2026/).
- Snapchat self-serve has no rate card or published benchmark, so every figure is an estimate — [RankRush KSA](https://rankrushksa.com/blog/social-media/snapchat-meta-tiktok-ksa-benchmarks/) (search summary).
- **Global Snapchat 2025:** CPM $8.39-9.63 (31-36 SAR), cost per landing click $0.90-1.02 (3.4-3.8 SAR), CPI $1.85-3.20 (6.9-12 SAR). A 2026 analysis of 2,431 campaigns found a median CPC of $0.84 (3.15 SAR), median CTR 1.04%, Awareness CPM $5.84 and Conversions CPM $27.10 **[third-party trackers]** — [Enrich Labs Snapchat guide 2026](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/snapchat-ads-complete-guide-2026); [novoads Snapchat cost](https://novoads.ai/en/blog/snapchat-ads-cost); [ThePricer](https://www.thepricer.org/how-much-do-snapchat-ads-cost/).
- Arabic practitioner rule of thumb: pause or replace an ad if **CPC > 2 SAR or CTR < 0.8%** **[practitioner heuristic]** — [aismartprompts Saudi Snapchat/TikTok guide](https://aismartprompts.com/articles/snapchat-tiktok-marketing-saudi-business-guide).
- **TikTok KSA:** CPM $3.80-7.40 (14-28 SAR), CPC $0.30-0.75 (1.1-2.8 SAR), CPL $10-25 (37-94 SAR) — [Stackmatix TikTok cost by country 2026](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-by-country-2026). Another source puts TikTok KSA CPC at $0.20-0.80 and CPA at $8-25 (30-94 SAR) — [The Hovi, performance marketing KSA/UAE 2026](https://thehovi.com/blog/agency-insights/performance-marketing-ksa-uae-strategies-roi-2026). The two sources give conflicting CPMs ($4.50-9.00 vs $3.80-7.40). The Hovi recommends **15,000-30,000 SAR/month** for sustained TikTok results — [The Hovi TikTok KSA guide 2026](https://thehovi.com/blog/industry-guides/tiktok-marketing-saudi-arabia-business-guide-2026). **[vendor estimates]**
- **Real-estate proxy, Meta KSA:** 30-90 SAR per form lead and 80-200 SAR per qualified lead for mid-market residential; 150-350 SAR per qualified lead for premium — [ITD Growth Labs real-estate KSA playbook 2026](https://itdgrowthlabs.com/resources/performance-marketing-playbook-for-real-estate-saudi-arabia.php). A second source gives 200-600 SAR — [Scalefield Lab Snapchat vs TikTok vs Meta](https://www.scalefieldlab.com/insights/snapchat-tiktok-meta-ads-uae-saudi). TikTok GCC real estate: form CPL ~16-75 SAR, with qualified CPL "materially higher" — [Salman Gul, TikTok ads real estate GCC](https://salmangul.com/tiktok-ads-for-real-estate-gcc/). **[vendor estimates]**
- Snap launched an "App Power Pack" in KSA and claims it drives up to 25% growth in app installs **[platform claim]** — [The Brand Berries](https://thebrandberries.com/?p=24968).
- **Landing-page conversion:** the Unbounce 2024 Conversion Benchmark Report puts the all-industry median at **6.6%** (41,000+ pages, 57M conversions) **[confirmed, first-party large sample]**. **Events & Entertainment median is 12.3%**, the highest of any industry. Real Estate is 2.7%. The 12.3% figure rests on a secondary table citing Unbounce Q4 2024 — [Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/?trk=s-bl); [ClickMinded landing-page benchmarks 2026](https://www.clickminded.com/landing-page-conversion-benchmark/); [MarketingProfs 2024](https://marketingprofs.com/charts/2024/52374/landing-page-conversion-benchmarks). In the 2020 report, the top-quartile median was 25% — [Unbounce 2020](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report-2020/).

### Inferences
- **[inference] Base case for 10,000 SAR/month, blended across the three platforms:** CPM ~18 SAR gives ~555k impressions. CTR ~0.8-1.0% gives ~4,500-5,500 clicks (CPC ~1.8-2.2 SAR). Click-to-signup/lead at 5-12% gives **~250-650 signups/leads/month**, or a CPL/CPS of **~15-40 SAR**. App install campaigns at ~7-12 SAR CPI give ~800-1,400 installs, but registration-after-install is typically a fraction of that; local data is needed to set that rate.
- **[inference]** A conservative case (CPM 25 SAR, CTR 0.6%, CVR 4%) gives ~400k impressions, ~2,400 clicks and **~95 leads**, or ~105 SAR/lead. A scenario band of roughly 100-650 leads/month is defensible.
- **[inference]** Treat a "quote request with a date and budget" as the qualified lead. Real-estate data suggests a qualified lead costs about 2-3x a form lead.

### Gaps
- No Saudi data was found for the wedding/events or beauty categories. No official Snap/TikTok/Meta MENA case study with CPL for event services was found.
- No Saudi CPI figure from a primary source was found, nor an install-to-registration rate for Saudi service apps.

## 2. Lead-to-booking conversion in wedding/events

### Takeaway
Across wedding marketplaces, about 10-20% of inquiries become bookings, so it takes about 5-10 inquiries per booking. Premium or high-ticket services convert lower (5-10%), and very fast responses can lift conversion a lot.

### Cited Findings
- **Zola marketplace (2023):** average inquiry-to-booking conversion of **18%**, from its own internal data **[first-party platform data]** — figure via search summary of [WeddingPro / industry sources](https://pros.weddingpro.com/?p=38780) (the page could not be fetched; attribution comes from the search summary).
- **Easy Weddings (Australia):** a healthy inquiry-to-booking rate for active vendors is **10-20%**. Premium vendors (>$3,000) see **5-10%**. Conversion improves once a vendor has more than 11 reviews **[platform guidance]** — [Easy Weddings, The Invisible Funnel](https://www.easyweddings.com.au/pro-education/qa-from-our-recent-webinar/).
- Wedding planners convert **8-15%** with 12-24h response times and **25-40%** with responses under 5 minutes. A WeddingWire survey is cited as finding that **74% of couples book the first vendor who responds** with helpful, personalized information **[vendor marketing claim]** — [Conferbot](https://www.conferbot.com/blog/chatbot-for-wedding-planners).
- The average venue response time in Australia is **42 hours** — [Real Weddings AU](https://www.realweddings.com.au/rw/blog/how-fast-your-venue-responding-enquiries-heres-how-you-compare/).
- WeddingPro and Moposa recommend tracking enquiry → viewing/consultation → proposal → booking, broken down by source — [Moposa, 5 metrics](https://moposa.com/insider-view/5-metrics-every-wedding-venue-should-track); [WeddingPro](https://pros.weddingpro.com/?p=37819).

### Inferences
- **[inference]** Model **qualified quote request → confirmed booking = 10% (low) / 15% (base) / 20-25% (high, with fast vendor response SLAs)**. Model **signup → quote request = 20-40%**; this is my assumption, not a benchmark.
- **[inference] Base case:** ~400 signups/month × 30% gives ~120 quote requests; × 15% gives **~18 bookings/month**. Conservative: 100 leads × 20% × 10% ≈ **2 bookings/month**. Optimistic: 650 × 40% × 20% ≈ **52/month**. So 10k SAR/month of ads should yield roughly **2-50 bookings/month**, with ~10-20 a realistic central estimate. That implies a paid CAC per booking of roughly **500-1,000 SAR** in the base case.
- **[inference]** In a marketplace, one wedding can generate several vendor bookings (hall, photographer, kosha, catering, invitations). The bookings-per-event multiplier is a key lever.

### Gaps
- No Saudi or GCC inquiry-to-booking data was found. No hotel event-sales conversion data was found (the Indigo Digital venue benchmarks page could not be fetched).
- No public CAC or payback figures were found for wedding marketplaces (The Knot, Bridebook, Zola).

## 3. Booking lead time and seasonality (Saudi)

### Takeaway
The Saudi evidence is thin. Regional planners advise starting 6-12 months ahead, and popular halls are booked months to a year out. US couples' engagements average 15 months. No Saudi survey of booking lead times was found.

### Cited Findings
- A regional event planner says 6-12 months is the ideal planning start — [Harper's Bazaar Arabia](https://www.harpersbazaararabia.com/bride/wedding-planning/how-to-plan-a-wedding-according-to-one-of-the-regions-top-event-planners).
- Most Saudi venues are booked "months, or even a year" in advance — [Arabia Weddings, Saudi planners](https://www.arabiaweddings.com/saudi-arabia/planners); [Arabia Weddings tips](https://www.arabiaweddings.com/tips/top-7-wedding-planning-advice-arab-brides).
- US: average engagement length is **15 months**, stable since 2022 (16,956 couples married in 2024) **[confirmed, large survey]** — [The Knot 2025 Real Weddings Study](https://www.theknot.com/content/wedding-data-insights/real-weddings-study); [The Knot, average engagement length](https://www.theknot.com/content/average-engagement-length).
- Generic venue guidance: popular ballrooms in peak season are booked 12-18 months out; small or off-season venues need only a few months — [Ocus venue guide](https://vtour1.ocus.com/post/how-far-in-advance-to-book-a-wedding-venue).
- **Ramadan:** hotels and wedding halls earn significant revenue from iftar and sohour events during Ramadan, which implies the halls are repurposed rather than used for weddings — [PR Newswire, Saudi celebration and wedding hall market report](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/saudi-arabia-celebration-and-wedding-hall-market-2017-2021---rising-number-of-marriages-and-corporate-649054263.html).
- In Dubai (a proxy), the Eid season is a popular time for weddings — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/going-out/society/high-wedding-bills-sour-celebrations-during-eid-1.1065052). One unverified vendor page claims October-March holds most Saudi events — [Mukaab Events seasonal page](https://mukaabevents.pages.dev/planning/seasonal-considerations/). An unsourced blog claims Saudi wedding-hall demand fell about 50% — [blog](https://myblog-times.netlify.app/articles/50-drop-in-saudi-wedding-hall-demand-economic-cultural-and-demographic-shifts) (treat with caution).
- Monthly GASTAT marriage counts vary noticeably between months; for example, May 2019 had only 512 Saudi marriage contracts in the Eastern Province — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/569096); [Saudi Gazette](https://www.saudigazette.com.sa/article/572738).

### Inferences
- **[inference]** For modelling, assume a **3-12 month lag** between first signup and the event, and a **1-6 month lag** between quote request and confirmed booking (halls and photographers are booked first, smaller vendors later). In month 1-3, many leads will be for events 6+ months away, so revenue lags spend.
- **[inference]** Suggested seasonality multipliers to validate locally: Ramadan ~0.4-0.6x for weddings (though high for iftar/corporate events). Shawwal after Eid al-Fitr and summer school holidays (June-August) are peaks at ~1.3-1.5x. Dhul-Hijjah around Eid al-Adha is a secondary peak. The Hijri calendar shifts about 11 days earlier each year, so Ramadan moves through the Gregorian calendar (it falls around February-March in 2026-2027).

### Gaps
- No Saudi survey was found on booking lead times or when couples start searching. No Eastern Province hall-occupancy data was found. Monthly GASTAT tables (stats.gov.sa) were not reachable from this session.

## 4. Word-of-mouth, referrals and invitation viral loops

### Takeaway
Word of mouth is the leading or near-leading vendor discovery channel: about 55% of couples in one 2016 US survey, and 70-90% of bookings for some established vendors. No public data quantifies guest-to-user conversion for digital invitation or wedding-website products. Any viral coefficient must be an assumption.

### Cited Findings
- 2016 survey with Destination I Do magazine: **55% of couples found vendors via word of mouth**, 38% via magazine ads (dated) — [Destination I Do](https://destinationido.com/real-weddings/blog-2016-10-12-survey-says-youre-paying-for-your-wedding-2).
- Easy Weddings: ~**21%** of couples found their celebrant through friends or family. Couples start on platforms and Google, then social media, then recommendations from friends and past couples (2025 survey) — [Easy Weddings, factors driving choice](https://www.easyweddings.com.au/pro-education/factors-that-drive-couples-to-choose-suppliers); [Easy Weddings celebrants](https://www.easyweddings.com.au/pro-education/how-couples-choose-their-celebrants).
- Individual photographers report **71%** and **90%** of bookings from referrals (anecdotes) — [SLR Lounge](https://www.slrlounge.com/how-to-get-wedding-photography-clients/?refc=jmgant); [The Every Last Detail](https://theeverylastdetail.com/pro-posts/how-to-encourage-word-of-mouth-referrals/).
- WeddingWire: nearly **80%** of couples consider reviews very important when hiring a vendor — via [The Every Last Detail](https://theeverylastdetail.com/pro-posts/how-to-encourage-word-of-mouth-referrals/) (secondary).
- Couples are "331% more likely" to rely on word of mouth from friends than on advertising (Chatter Matters; method unverified) — [SmartCompany](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/marketing/word-of-mouth-marketing-planning-wedding/).
- **Zola:** the free wedding website is the acquisition hook, with retargeting after signup driving registry creation (third-party ad analysis) — [adlibrary Zola](https://adlibrary.com/brands/zola). Zola uses TikTok so couples know the brand before they get engaged — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/how-zola-aims-to-communicate-expanded-wedding-offerings-boost-brand-awareness-as-nuptials-make-a-comeback/). An early figure claims "7 million+ guests attended a Zola wedding" (circa 2018, unverified) — [Latka podcast](https://nathanlatkathetop.libsyn.com/839-marketplace-zola-wedding-registry-passes-120m-gmv-run-rate).

### Inferences
- **[inference] Viral-loop model (assumption-driven):** each wedding sends a digital invitation to ~300-600 guests. If ~1-2% of guests click a "powered by مناسبتي" link and ~5-10% of those sign up, that is ~0.15-1.2 signups per wedding. Only a small share of those will have an event within 12 months, given the unmarried age group and other events such as graduations and engagements. So the invitation loop gives a **k-factor of maybe 0.05-0.3 bookings per booking**: meaningful, but not self-sustaining virality.
- **[inference] Direct referral:** if 30-50% of satisfied clients recommend the service and ~10-20% of those recommendations convert within 12 months, each booking produces ~0.05-0.1 extra bookings. Combined with the invite loop, a k of ~0.1-0.4 means organic bookings add roughly **10-65%** on top of paid bookings by month 12. The effect compounds with a lag of 3-9 months because of event lead times.
- **[inference]** Saudi wedding guest lists are large and gender-segregated (separate men's and women's invitations), which plausibly increases invitation exposure. Snapchat and WhatsApp sharing makes the loop cheaper than in the US.

### Gaps
- No published data was found on guest-to-user conversion for Paperless Post, Joy, Zola or Saudi digital-invitation apps.
- No NPS norms were found for the wedding industry. The searches did not return reliable figures.

## 5. Saudi and Eastern Province marriage market size

### Takeaway
The latest GASTAT-published total found is **150,117 marriage contracts in 2020** (+8.9% vs 2019's 137,918). One secondary source claims GASTAT has published no newer annual total. The Eastern Province had ~1,656 contracts in one 2019 month, which points to roughly **15,000-20,000 marriages per year** there [inference].

### Cited Findings
- 2020: **150,117** marriage contracts (Ministry of Justice records, published by GASTAT), +8.9% vs 2019. Al-Jouf had the highest marriage rate **[confirmed, official]** — [GASTAT Marriage and Divorce Statistics 2020 (PDF)](https://www.stats.gov.sa/documents/20117/2435265/Marriage+and+Divorce+Statistics+2020+EN.pdf/239cf095-5ca3-114d-2373-1c262a4c8bdd?t=1735073651142) (from the search summary; the PDF could not be fetched); [Saudi Gazette](https://www.saudigazette.com.sa/article/604930/SAUDI-ARABIA/89-increase-in-Saudi-marriages-in-2020); [Ajel](https://english.ajel.sa/lifestyle/saudi-marriages-raised-by-8-9-in-2020).
- 2019: **137,918** contracts, down about 8% from 2018 **[confirmed]** — [GASTAT 2019 (PDF)](https://www.stats.gov.sa/documents/20117/1993170/marriage_and_divorce_statistics_2019_en_0.pdf/32f507b4-f65a-f879-762d-15710d1cc8ce?t=1735073552608).
- About 148,000 marriages in an earlier year (circa 2017) — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/1185846/{{). The Ministry of Justice once reported 144,436 contracts in a year, 88% via ma'thuns (year unclear) — [Al Riyadh](https://www.alriyadh.com/533906).
- **Eastern Province:** **1,656** contracts in one 2019 month (3rd after Makkah and Riyadh). In May 2019 there were **512** Saudi-national contracts versus 625 divorces — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/569096); [Saudi Gazette](https://www.saudigazette.com.sa/article/572738).
- A commentary site says GASTAT has published no marriage/divorce total since 2020 and that later circulating figures are "re-dated" 2020 data **[unverified]** — [vision2030.ai divorce rate](https://vision2030.ai/encyclopedia/divorce-rate-saudi-arabia/). An unofficial 2024 estimate of ~165,000 marriages is based on aggregated, unconfirmed data; do not rely on it — [RPubs](https://rstudio-pubs-static.s3.amazonaws.com/1366871_75044333de20469287ac972d0a5a6d07.html).

### Inferences
- **[inference]** National total ≈ 140-160k weddings per year. Eastern Province ≈ 1,300-1,700 per month, or **~15-20k per year** (about 11-13% of the national total, scaled from the 2019 monthly figure). Adding engagements (milka/khutba), graduations, births and corporate events multiplies the event count several times over.
- **[inference]** Capturing 15 paid bookings/month (~180/year) would be about 1% of Eastern Province weddings. That is plausible for year 1, and the market is not the binding constraint.

### Gaps
- Official 2021-2024 totals and the latest regional breakdown were not reachable (stats.gov.sa is blocked in this session). Recommend downloading GASTAT "إحصاءات الزواج والطلاق" and the Ministry of Justice statistical yearbook directly.
- Typical CAC and payback for wedding/event marketplaces: no public figures were found.
