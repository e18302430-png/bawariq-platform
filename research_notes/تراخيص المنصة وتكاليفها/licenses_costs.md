Research date: 2026-11-09. Method note: only web search was possible. Page fetches to alraedah.sa, twobirds.com and rulebook.sama.gov.sa failed because those hosts are outside this session's network allowlist, so most figures come from search-result snippets, not full-page reads. Status labels used below: **CONFIRMED** means a primary/official source or several independent reputable sources agree. **SECONDARY** means it comes from consultancy or service-provider blogs only. **ESTIMATE / UNVERIFIED** means the sources conflict or the figure is inferred.

## 1. Commercial Registration (السجل التجاري): sole establishment vs (single-person) LLC, fees, chamber, trade name, 2025 law changes, ISIC codes

### Takeaway
The new Commercial Registration Law and Trade Names Law took effect on 3 April 2025. A CR no longer has an expiry date or a "renewal". Instead, the data must be confirmed every year (التأكيد السنوي) on the Saudi Business Center (business.sa) within 12 months of issuance. The fee most often cited is SAR 200/year for a sole establishment's main CR. For an LLC it is about SAR 1,200/year (several secondary sources, not confirmed officially for the new law). Chamber of commerce membership is extra: about SAR 300-500/yr at the lowest class for a small sole establishment, and about SAR 2,000 for an LLC in older tables. The figures conflict between sources.

### Cited Findings
- **Effective date (CONFIRMED):** The Ministry of Commerce put the new Commercial Registry and Trade Name laws, with their executive regulations, into force on 3 April 2025 — [Argaam (EN)](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1802243); [LexisNexis, 3 Apr 2025](https://www.lexisnexis.com/blogs/ae-legal/b/insights/posts/ksa-new-commercial-registry-and-trade-names-systems-implemented). Al Tamimi (Dec 2024) says they were published in the Official Gazette on 4 Oct 2024 with a 180-day lead-in, and that "the registration will no longer have an expiration date" — [Al Tamimi](https://tamimi.com/news/new-commercial-registration-and-trade-name-laws-take-effect-in-2025).
- **Annual confirmation replaces renewal (CONFIRMED):** Data is confirmed electronically within 12 months of issuance. If the confirmation is late, the CR and related services are suspended 90 days after the due date. If it is not fixed within a year (fees plus any penalty), the CR is cancelled automatically. Fines go up to SAR 50,000 — [Mercator](https://www.mercator.net/our-thinking/latest-news/saudi-arabia-new-commercial-registration-and-trade-name-law/); [DWF, Apr 2025](https://dwfgroup.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2025/4/saudi-commercial-register-law-and-law-of-trade-names). Any change to registered data must be updated within 15 days — [Tanfeth guide](https://tanfeth.sa/new-commercial-registration-law-saudi-guide/); [HD Law Firm](https://hd-lawfirm.com.sa/blog/%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A/).
- **Annual confirmation fee (SECONDARY):** Several guides cite SAR 500 for a sole establishment and SAR 1,000-1,600 for companies depending on type — [Batic Firm](https://baticfirm.com/ar/%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9/); [Sighaty](https://sighaty.com/guides/cr-annual-confirmation.html); [TASGCC 2026](https://www.tasgcc.com/renew-commercial-registration/). This conflicts with the long-standing SAR 200/yr main CR fee (below). These "500 / 1,000-1,600" figures may bundle chamber or other fees. **UNVERIFIED: check the fee invoice on business.sa.**
- **CR issuance fee (CONFIRMED for the old regime, SECONDARY for 2026):** The Ministry of Commerce set SAR 200 per year for a main CR and SAR 100 for a branch CR — [Argaam (AR), MoC announcement](https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/1573059). 2026 guides repeat SAR 200/yr for a sole establishment. For LLCs they most often cite SAR 1,200/yr for the main CR and SAR 600 for a branch, but one source says SAR 800 — [Rukn Services 2026](https://rukn-services.com/%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A/); [Dafater 2026](https://dafater.sa/blog/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9/); [Afaq Al-Syola 2026](https://blog.afaqalsyola.com.sa/%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9/). One 2026 blog suggests SAR 1,200-2,000 under the new law but calls it "indicative". Disregard that figure.
- **Trade name reservation (SECONDARY):** Instant reservation of a personal (owner-name) trade name is said to be free. An "innovative" (coined) name such as "مناسبتي" carries a "nominal" fee, with no amount found — [Rukn Services](https://rukn-services.com/%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A/). Under the new Trade Names Law, a reservation lasts a limited period and can be extended once. Names may be Arabic, transliterated, English or alphanumeric — [Mercator](https://www.mercator.net/our-thinking/latest-news/saudi-arabia-new-commercial-registration-and-trade-name-law/).
- **Chamber of commerce membership (CONFLICTING):**
  - An official MoC consultation proposed fees by headcount: Category 1 (500+ workers) SAR 10,000; Category 2 (21-499) SAR 2,500/3,000; Category 3 (5-20) SAR 1,500/2,000. It also listed the earlier class-based fees (Excellent class SAR 10,000; First class SAR 5,000) — [MoC news](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/News/Pages/15-01-18-01.aspx). This item is old, and it is unclear whether the proposal was adopted.
  - The Abha Chamber FAQ lists First class SAR 5,000, Second 2,000, Third 1,000, Fourth 700, Fifth 500 and Sixth 200 — [Abha Chamber FAQ](https://www.abhacci.org.sa/FAQ).
  - 2026 commercial guides put LLCs at about SAR 2,000 (Second class) and small sole establishments at SAR 300-500 (Fourth class) — [Capsulaa 2026](https://www.capsulaa.sa/saudi-arabia-services/%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%BA%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A5%D9%84/); [Mqalaty](https://mqalaty.net/%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%83-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%BA%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9/).
  - Chamber membership is issued and renewed electronically together with the CR, through the ministry's subscriptions portal — [Abha Chamber FAQ](https://www.abhacci.org.sa/FAQ).
- **ISIC codes (NOT CONFIRMED):** ISIC 823 is "convention and trade show organisers" — [SSIC 823](https://industry-codes.com/ssic/823-convention-trade-show-concert-and-other-event-organisers). Balady has an activity lookup by ISIC code — [Balady activity lookup](https://apps.balady.gov.sa/LicenseStandard/Default?sid=7010). I found no Saudi source that gives the exact 6-digit code for "تنظيم الحفلات والمناسبات" or for "online marketplace / brokerage".

### Inferences
- **Lean sole establishment, year 1 (ESTIMATE):** CR SAR 200, plus chamber about SAR 300-700, plus a trade-name fee (likely small). Total roughly **SAR 500-1,000**. If the "SAR 500 annual confirmation" figure is a separate charge, the recurring cost would rise.
- **Single-person LLC, year 1 (ESTIMATE):** CR about SAR 1,200, plus chamber about SAR 2,000, plus articles of association publication/notary costs (not researched). Total roughly **SAR 3,200-4,000** before lawyer fees.
- **Likely activity set (to verify in the business.sa activity selector):**
  - Organizing parties and events (the 823x / 9000x family)
  - Retail sale via internet / e-commerce platforms (the 4791 family)
  - Commission agents / brokerage (the 4610 family, "الوكلاء بالعمولة / الوساطة")
  - Possibly printing or digital design for invitations, or web portals (the 6312 family)
  - These codes are from background knowledge, not confirmed by a Saudi source.
- **Entity choice:** An LLC gives limited liability. That matters because the platform will hold client deposits and carry vendor-default risk. A sole establishment exposes personal assets.

### Gaps
- No official 2025-2026 MoC fee table was retrieved. The exact annual-confirmation fee, the LLC CR fee under the new law, and the coined trade-name fee are unconfirmed.
- The current chamber class and fee table (Eastern Province / Asharqia Chamber) was not found.
- The exact Saudi ISIC codes were not found.

## 2. Does an events/wedding platform need a General Entertainment Authority (GEA), Tourism, Culture or Municipalities license?

### Takeaway
The GEA regime covers **public** entertainment events and live shows. Private events whose invitations are not addressed to the general public (for example a closed family wedding) are reported to be excluded from the Entertainment Activities rules. A matching/booking platform for private weddings and occasions most likely needs **no GEA license**. That changes if it ever organizes or sells tickets to public events, and then penalties can reach SAR 1 million.

### Cited Findings
- **Private events excluded:** "Events and private shows whose invitation is not directed to the general public are not considered entertainment activities subject to the system." Licensing is mandatory for organizing public events, with fines up to SAR 1 million — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/local/na/2255699).
- **Permits for public events:** Temporary entertainment events such as concerts and plays need a per-event entertainment permit. GEA services require a valid CR with the relevant activity added — [GEA Investor Guide](https://apieims.gea.gov.sa/api/documents/ViewTemplateFile?code=1); [business.sa: entertainment event facilities operating licence](https://business.sa/eservices/details/b6feced1-f55c-4901-6456-08dc82e29bc9); [Meras](https://meras.gov.sa/ar/licenses/general-authority-for-entertainment/ترخيص-مرافق-الفعاليات-الترفيهية-1/).
- **Proposed GEA fees (unknown if final):** SAR 2,500 for the first day in category 1 and SAR 1,500 in category 2, plus SAR 250 / SAR 150 per extra day. An older report mentions annual fees and 5% on tickets — [Al-Madina](https://www.al-madina.com/article/1009959/%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%81%D9%8A%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B1).
- **History:** GEA moved event licensing online in 2017, using the CR number to register — [SPA](https://www.spa.gov.sa/w536560).

### Inferences
- **Status: CONDITIONAL.** No GEA license is needed for a pure intermediary for private weddings and engagements, as long as the venue/vendor holds its own licenses. One is needed if مناسبتي itself organizes public or ticketed events, or events with paid public performers.
- The venues (halls, istirahat) are responsible for their own Balady licenses and civil defense certificates. The platform should collect and verify vendor CR and license numbers when onboarding them.
- Tourism (MT) licenses cover accommodation and travel, and Culture covers performing-arts permits. Neither appears to apply to the core model, but this was not specifically confirmed.

### Gaps
- No official GEA statement on whether a *platform* or *private-wedding coordinator* needs a license was found.
- Current final GEA fee schedule not found.
- The on-site check-in staffing model was not researched against GEA or labor rules. **Needs a lawyer.**

## 3. Municipal (Balady) license, virtual office, civil defense

### Takeaway
Balady commercial licenses attach to a physical activity location, which must be selected when applying, and come bundled with a civil defense safety permit. Online-only businesses without premises reportedly need only a CR plus e-commerce authentication, with no Balady license. This comes from a non-official source.

### Cited Findings
- The Balady "issue commercial license" service is electronic. It requires choosing the activity location and includes the Civil Defense safety permit — [MOMAH e-service](https://momah.gov.sa/en/e-services/issuing-commercial-license).
- "E-stores without a physical location need only a CR and e-commerce authentication; a municipal license is not required" — [Daleel Services (non-official)](https://www.daleel.services/service/issue-municipal-license/).
- Balady licenses can be renewed online without visiting the municipality — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/qwtvy89).

### Inferences
- **Status:** Balady is **CONDITIONAL**, required only once a physical office or warehouse exists. The civil defense certificate is **CONDITIONAL** in the same way and comes bundled with Balady.
- A virtual or co-working office address may be required by banks or payment gateways even if Balady does not require one. The cost is not researched; see the gaps.
- **National Address (العنوان الوطني, Saudi Post/SPL):** usually required for CR, banks and gateways. Registering an individual national address is generally free (background knowledge, not sourced here).

### Gaps
- Balady license fee formula for an office (it depends on area and signage) was not retrieved.
- Virtual-office prices in Dammam/Khobar were not found.

## 4. E-commerce authentication (Maroof status 2025-2026)

### Takeaway
Maroof has been replaced. The Ministry of Commerce directs e-stores and platforms to obtain an **e-commerce authentication certificate (توثيق التجارة الإلكترونية)** on the Saudi Business Center platform. The certificate is issued electronically and **free of charge**.

### Cited Findings
- The MoC asks individual and business e-store owners to authenticate on "منصة الأعمال" (SBC) instead of Maroof. The certificate is issued electronically for free. Steps: search "توثيق", choose "إصدار توثيق التجارة الإلكترونية" and enter the store data — [Salla Help](https://help.salla.sa/article/%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%AB%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF/poppbr8akg1iqlr2798u8gfz); [Salla Help 2](https://help.salla.sa/article/توثيق-متجرك-على-منصة-الأعمال-بدلا-من-معروف/gno9ilestioyspm61yu5buzp).
- Verified stores can be looked up on SBC by authentication number, unified number, CR, freelance document number or store name — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/1DmiAPF).

### Inferences
- **Status: MANDATORY** for a platform selling services online in KSA (E-Commerce Law). The certificate renews periodically, but the exact renewal period was not found. The E-Commerce Law also requires disclosures (CR number, contact details, terms, cancellation/refund policy) on the site. A lawyer should draft the T&Cs, the refund policy for deposits, and the vendor agreement.

### Gaps
- Renewal period of the authentication certificate not found.
- PDPL obligations were not researched. The platform will hold guest lists and phone numbers for invitations and check-in. The Personal Data Protection Law (SDAIA) has applied since Sept 2023 and may require a privacy policy, possible registration in the national register of controllers, and consent for WhatsApp/SMS invitations. **Needs a lawyer.**

## 5. VAT (ZATCA) and e-invoicing (FATOORA)

### Takeaway
VAT registration with ZATCA is free. It is **mandatory** above SAR 375,000 of taxable supplies in 12 months and **voluntary** from SAR 187,500. Registering late carries a SAR 10,000 penalty. Once registered, e-invoicing Phase 1 applies at once, and Phase 2 (integration) comes in waves. Approved software typically costs about SAR 600-1,500/yr at the low end; the one data point found is AmalERP.

### Cited Findings
- Thresholds: mandatory above SAR 375,000, voluntary from SAR 187,500. Registration is due within 30 days, with a SAR 10,000 late-registration penalty — [Tasis Partners](https://www.tasispartners.com/en/vat-registration-saudi-arabia); [Wafeq](https://www.wafeq.com/en-sa/tax-and-reporting/vat-registration-in-saudi-arabia-zatca-step-by-step-guide-for-businesses); [Expandway 2026](https://expandway.sa/vat-in-saudi-arabia/).
- E-invoicing waves: Phase 1 has applied to all VAT-registered businesses since Dec 2021. Wave 24 (above SAR 375k) had a 30 June 2026 deadline. Wave 25 (above SAR 187.5k) has a deadline of 1 Feb 2027 — [AmalERP](https://amalerp.com/sa/zatca-e-invoicing); [Synergy](https://synergystrat.com/zatca-vat-compliance-guide-2026/).
- Software prices: AmalERP's ZATCA Phase 1 plan is SAR 599/yr and its Phase 2 plan SAR 1,499/yr — [AmalERP](https://amalerp.com/sa/zatca-e-invoicing). Wafeq requires its Plus plan or higher for Phase 2, price not retrieved — [Wafeq](https://www.wafeq.com/en-sa/tax-and-reporting/zatca-compliance-requirements). Qoyod e-invoice FAQ — [Qoyod](https://www.qoyod.com/en/learn/e-invoice-faq/).

### Inferences
- **Key tax risk (needs an accountant):** whether the platform's turnover is counted as only its **commission** (acting as agent) or the **full event value** (acting as principal). If it is the full value, the SAR 375k threshold would be crossed quickly. Agent-style contracts usually keep turnover at the commission level.
- **Status:** VAT is CONDITIONAL (turnover threshold). Zakat/income filing with ZATCA is MANDATORY for every CR holder (background knowledge).

### Gaps
- Qoyod, Daftra and Wafeq 2026 plan prices not retrieved (typical market range is roughly SAR 100-300/month; unverified).

## 6. Payment gateways and SAMA (holding client deposits, paying vendors)

### Takeaway
**This is the highest legal risk in the model.** Under the Law of Payments and Payment Services, nobody may provide payment services in KSA without a SAMA license. SAMA's e-commerce rules treat a **pooled account that receives and keeps customers' funds** as a regulated payment service. Collecting client deposits by bank transfer into the platform's own account and paying vendors after the event looks like that kind of pooled-funds activity.
Safer structures:
- (a) Client pays the vendor directly, and the platform invoices only its commission.
- (b) Use a SAMA-licensed gateway or PSP with marketplace/split-settlement, so funds never sit in مناسبتي's account.
- (c) Use a lawyer-drafted commercial-agent model, though its sufficiency is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- The law states: "A person may not operate a payment system or provide payment services in the Kingdom unless licensed by SAMA." It requires client funds to be segregated from own funds — [Law of Payments and Payment Services, official translation (MISA, 2025)](https://misa.gov.sa/app/uploads/2025/08/Law-of-Payments-and-Payment-Services.pdf).
- SAMA's e-commerce rules: activities that require a pooled account to deposit and keep customers' funds fall within payment services and need a SAMA license. A Payment Technical Service Provider needs no license but is limited to linking and technical support, and may not finalize settlement or deposit funds to merchants — [SAMA Rulebook: Rules for Dealing with E-Commerce Payment Service and Support Providers](https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/rules-dealing-e-commerce-payment-service-and-support-providers-0) (a 2019-era rule; summary via search, page not fetched).
- Bird & Bird (2026): the position changes if a company "controls settlement, holds customer or merchant funds, operates a wallet, provides payment services to third-party merchants" — [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/saudi-arabia/saudi-financial-services-understanding-the-regulatory-perimeter-of-sama-and-the-cma).
- SAMA PSP Regulatory Guidelines (Jan 2020) — [SAMA PDF](https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-sa/payment/Documents/Payment_Services_Provider_Regulatory_Guidelines_.pdf).
- **Gateway pricing (UNVERIFIED, sources conflict):**
  - **Moyasar:** setup and monthly fees reportedly free, with mada at 1.5% + SAR 1. Other sources say SAR 0-200/month or a flat 2.5%. Moyasar says pricing comes from its sales team.
  - **Tap:** "Go" plan free, mada about 1.75% + SAR 1. Other sources say 2.5% + SAR 1 and cards about 2.85% + SAR 0.30.
  - **HyperPay:** setup about SAR 1,500 and about SAR 250/month, with rates of 2.5-3.5%.
  - **Geidea:** online setup generally free.
  - A 2026 comparison says none of these publish API rates, so quotes are needed — [Raghdan 2025](https://raghdan.sa/en/news/payment-gateways-in-saudi-arabia-guide-2025-requirements-fees-registration-steps-and-comprehensive-comparison/); [ijjad 2026](https://www.ijjad.com/payment-gateway-fees-saudi-arabia-2026); [Startupik 2026](https://startupik.com/saudi-payment-gateway-fees-startups-2026/); [Logio Legion 2026](https://logiolegion.com/blogs/tap-payments-vs-hyperpay-vs-moyasar-saudi-arabia-2026); [HyperPay merchant guide](https://www.hyperpay.com/blog/payment-gateway-saudi-arabia/).

### Inferences
- **Status:** A SAMA PSP license for مناسبتي itself is not realistic for a lean startup (it requires capital and a long process; amounts not researched). Treat **"no pooled holding of client money"** as the design requirement.
- Gateway onboarding typically requires an active CR, e-commerce authentication, a business bank account in the entity's name, the owner's ID, the national address, a live website with T&Cs and a refund policy, and the CR activity matching the business. These are typical requirements and were not individually verified per gateway.
- Ask each gateway in writing for "marketplace / split payments / delayed settlement to sub-merchants" support and fees. Sub-merchant KYC would fall on the vendors.
- **Needs a lawyer:** the deposit/escrow model, the commercial-agent clause, refund liabilities, and whether bank-transfer collection into the company account is acceptable.

### Gaps
- Gateways' own marketplace/split features and fees were not confirmed.
- SAMA licensing capital and fees for a PSP were not researched.

## 7. Trademark at SAIP ("مناسبتي" name/logo)

### Takeaway
Saudi applications are single-class. Official fees are most consistently reported as **SAR 1,000 filing + SAR 500 publication + SAR 5,000 registration/certificate = SAR 6,500 per class**, with protection for 10 years. Some sources give a different publication fee.

### Cited Findings
- SAR 6,500 per class (1,000 filing, 500 publication, 5,000 registration). Three classes cost SAR 19,500 — [Analytix](https://analytix.sa/?p=24891); [TMarkMetric June 2026](https://tmarkmetric.com/guides/saudi-arabia-trademark).
- The SAR 5,000 registration/certificate fee is confirmed by the Chambers "Doing Business In 2025" guide — [Chambers 2025](https://gpg-pdf.chambers.com/doing-business-in-2025/730).
- Conflicts: other sources give a publication fee of SAR 1,500 or SAR 575, and one gives a filing fee of about SAR 1,500 — [blog 2025](https://business-setup-services-in-saudi.blogspot.com/2025/06/trademark-registration-in-saudi-arabia.html); [Motaded](https://motaded.com.sa/platforms/saip).

### Inferences
- **Status: OPTIONAL but strongly recommended**, especially before marketing spend. "مناسبتي" is a generic-sounding Arabic word, so it may be hard to register on its own. A distinctive logo or composite mark is more registrable. Run a SAIP search first (a lawyer or agent is advisable).
- **Priority classes:**
  - 35: online marketplace / advertising
  - 41: organizing events and parties
  - 42: SaaS / platform
  - 45 is optional (social services, wedding planning sits partly here)
  - A lean option is 35 + 41 only = SAR 13,000.
- **Timeline:** typically a few months (examination, then publication, then a 60-day opposition period). Background knowledge, not sourced here.

### Gaps
- The official SAIP fee schedule page could not be accessed.

## 8. Domain (.sa / .com.sa) and National Address

### Takeaway
.sa domains need a local presence (CR) or a Saudi trademark. Saudi resellers charge about SAR 99/yr. Registration runs through the Saudi NIC (nic.sa, under CST).

### Cited Findings
- To register a .sa domain you need a seat or representative office in KSA, or a valid Saudi trademark — [101domain](https://www.101domain.com/sa-information-help.htm); [CST service page](https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/services/saudi-domain-name-registration); [nic.sa regulations](https://nic.sa/en/help/regulation).

## 9. Employees: GOSI, Qiwa, Muqeem, Nitaqat; Freelance option

### Takeaway
GOSI registration is mandatory once anyone is hired.
- **Saudis who first joined on or after 3 Jul 2024:** pension contribution of 9% each side, rising 0.5 points a year to 11% each, on top of SANED and occupational hazards.
- **Expats:** the employer pays 2% occupational hazards only.
- **Expat levy:** SAR 800/month per expat above the number of Saudis, SAR 700/month up to that number. Entities with 9 or fewer workers that include a full-time owner registered with GOSI are exempt for 2 expats (4 if there is another full-time Saudi) until about Jan 2027.
- **Freelance document (freelance.sa):** issued electronically. One source says it is free, but whether event organizing is a permitted field is unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- GOSI new-law rates and the 1 Jul 2025 increase — [Teamed](https://www.teamed.global/gulf-employment-law/saudi-gosi-and-2025-amendments); [Analytix 2026](https://analytix.sa/gosi-registration-in-saudi-arabia-complete-guide-for-employers-2026/); [Motaded GOSI](https://motaded.com.sa/platforms/gosi).
- Levy of SAR 800/700, the small-entity exemption until 14 Sha'ban 1448 AH, and red-band entities being unable to renew permits — [Saur Agency calculator](https://sauragency.com/en/tools/iqama-renewal-calculator). One secondary source puts the combined work permit cost at about SAR 9,700/yr — [Noblecore](https://noblecoreksa.com/iqama-fees-breakdown-saudi/); [nnroad](https://nnroad.com/blog/saudi-arabia-work-visa/).
- Freelance document: MHRSD via freelance.sa, Absher login, up to 5 documents for different skills, renewal on the platform, and one source says no fees — [Sabbar](https://sabbar.com/blog/freelance-work-document-terms); [Qoyod](https://www.qoyod.com/en/blog/labor-market/what-is-a-freelance-work-document-sa/); [Wdeftksa](https://www.wdeftksa.com/sa/guides/freelance).

### Inferences
- **Status:** CONDITIONAL, applies only on hiring. Temporary event-day check-in staff, if hired directly, raise labor-law and GOSI questions. Using vendor or agency staff or gig contractors keeps it lean. **Needs a lawyer or HR advisor.**
- The freelance document is not a substitute for a CR here. The platform holds money, contracts with vendors and needs a gateway, and gateways and the SBC e-commerce authentication generally expect a CR or legal entity. It could cover the founder's personal services only.
- Qiwa and Muqeem accounts are free to open (background knowledge). Muqeem is needed only for expats.

### Gaps
- Exact current freelance-document fee and its list of eligible professions not confirmed.
- Asharqia-specific Nitaqat band thresholds for an "events" activity not researched.

## 10. Business bank account; total first-year cost estimate

### Takeaway
No source with bank-specific requirements or fees was retrieved. Bank accounts typically require an active CR, the owner's national ID, the national address, a chamber membership certificate, and the articles of association for an LLC. The estimated first-year government-fee total is roughly **SAR 1,000-2,500 for a lean sole establishment** and **SAR 4,000-6,000 for a single-person LLC**, excluding trademark and professional fees.
